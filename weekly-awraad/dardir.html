Here the Seeker reads the following prayers of

SHAYKH SIDI AD-DARDĪR

54

اَللَّهُمَّ صَلِّ وَسَلِّمْ وَبَارِكْ عَلَى سَيِّدِنَا مُحَمَّدٍ عَلَى أٰلِهٖ عَدَدَ مَا فِي الْأَرْضِ وَالسَّمَآءِ .

Allāhumma ṣalli wa sallim wa bārik `alā Sayyidinā Muḥammadin, `Adada mā fī ‘l-Arḍi wa‘s-Samā’.

O Allah send prayers upon, salute and bless our Master Muḥammad and his Family the number of everything on the earth and in the sky!

وَصَلِّ وَسَلِّمْ وَبَارِكْ عَلَى سَيِّدِنَا مُحَمَّدٍ وَعَلَى أٰلِهٖ وَعَلَى جَمِيْعِ الْمَلٰٓئِكَةِ وَ الْأَنْبِيَآءِ .

Wa ṣalli wa sallim wa bārik `alā Sayyidinā Muḥammadin wa `alā jamī‘i ‘l-Malā’ikati wa ‘l-Anbiyā’.

and send prayers upon, salute and bless our Master Muḥammad and his Family and all the Angels and Prophets!

وَصَلِّ وَسَلِّمْ وَبَارِكْ عَلَى سَيِّدِنَا مُحَمَّدٍ وَعَلَى أٰلِهٖ وَعَلَى سَائِرِ الْعُلَمَاءِ وَالْأَوْلِيَآءِ .

Wa ṣalli wa sallim wa bārik `alā Sayyidinā Muḥammadin wa `alā ālihī, Wa `alā sā’iri ‘l-`Ulamā’i wa ‘l-Awliyā’.

and send prayers upon, salute and bless our Master Muḥammad and his Family and all the Scholars and Saints!

55

وَصَلِّ وَسَلِّمْ وَبَارِكْ عَلَى سَيِّدِنَا مُحَمَّدٍ وَعَلَى أٰلِهٖ صَلَاةً تَمْلَأُ سَائِرَ الْأَقْطَارِ وَالْأَرْجَآءِ .

Wa ṣalli wa sallim wa bārik `alā Sayyidinā Muḥammadin wa `alā Ālihī, Ṣalātan tamla’u sā’ira ‘l-aqṭāri wa ‘l-arjā’.

and send prayers upon, salute and bless our Master Muḥammad and his Family a prayer that fills all regions and zones

وَصَلِّ وَ سَلِّمْ وَبَارِكْ عَلَى سَيِّدِنَا مُحَمَّدٍ عَلَى أٰلِهٖ وَحَقِّقْنَا بِحَقَائِقِ الصِّفَاتِ وَالْأَسْمَآءِ .

Wa ṣalli wa sallim wa bārik `alā Sayyidinā Muḥammadin wa `alā Ālihī, Wa ḥaqqiqnā bi ḥaqā’iqi ‘ṣ-Ṣifāti wa al-Asmā’.

and send prayers upon, salute and bless our Master Muḥammad and his Family and substantiate in us the reality of (Your) names and attributes!

وَصَلِّ وَسَلِّمْ وَبَارِكْ عَلَى سَيِّدِنَا مُحَمَّدٍ وَعَلَى أٰلِهٖ وَاجْعَلْنَا مَعَ الَّذِيْنَ أَنْعَمْتَ عَلَيْهِمْ مِنَ النَّبِيّٖنَ وَالصِّدِّيْقِيْنَ وَالشُّهَدَاءِ .

Wa ṣalli wa sallim wa bārik `alā Sayyidinā Muḥammadin wa `alā Ālihī, Wa ‘j`alnā ma`a ‘lladhīna an`amta `alayhim mina ‘n-Nabiyyīna wa ‘ṣ-Ṣiddīqīna wa ‘sh-Shuhadā’.

and send prayers upon, salute and bless our Master Muḥammad and his Family and put us with those You favor among the Prophets, truthful and martyrs!

56

وَصَلِّ وَسَلِّمْ وَبَارِكْ عَلَى سَيِّدِنَا مُحَمَّدٍ وَعَلَى أٰلِهٖ صَلَاةً تَقِيْنَا بِهَا شَرَّ الْحُسَّادِ وَ الْأَعْدَاءِ .

Wa ṣalli wa sallim wa bārik `alā Sayyidinā Muḥammadin wa `alā Ālihī, Ṣalātan taqīnā bi hā sharra ‘l-ḥussādi wa ‘l-a`dā’.

and send prayers upon, salute and bless our Master Muḥammad and his Family a prayer protecting us from the evil of the envious and worst enemies!

اَللَّهُمَّ صَلِّ وَسَلِّمْ وَبَارِكْ عَلَى سَيِّدِنَا مُحَمَّدٍ وَعَلَى أٰلِهٖ النَّاطِقِ بِالصِّدْقِ وَالصَّوَابِ .

Allāhumma ṣalli wa sallim wa bārik `alā Sayyidinā Muḥammadin wa `alā Ālihī, An-Nāṭiqi bi ‘ṣ-ṣidqi wa ‘ṣ-ṣawāb.

O Allah send prayers upon, salute and bless our Master Muḥammad and his Family the truthful and accurate communicator!

وَصَلِّ وَسَلِّمْ وَبَارِكْ عَلَى سَيِّدِنَا مُحَمَّدٍ وَعَلَى أٰلِهٖ أَفْضَلَ مَنْ أُوْتِيَ الْحِكْمَةَ وَفَصْلَ الْخِطَابِ .

Wa ṣalli wa sallim wa bārik `alā Sayyidinā Muḥammadin wa `alā Ālihī, Afḍala man ūtiya ‘l-ḥikmata wa faṣla ‘l-khiṭāb.

and send prayers upon, salute and bless our Master Muḥammad and his Family the best of those given wisdom and decisive speech!

57

وَصَلِّ وَسَلِّمْ وَبَارِكْ عَلَى سَيِّدِنَا مُحَمَّدٍ وَعَلَى أٰلِهٖ بَابِ الْأَبْوَابِ وَلُبَابِ اللُّبَابِ .

Wa ṣalli wa sallim wa bārik `alā Sayyidinā Muḥammadin wa `alā Ālihī, Bābi ‘l-Abwābi wa Lubābi ‘l-Lubāb.

and send prayers upon, salute and bless our Master Muḥammad and his Family, the door of domains and prime of intellects!

وَصَلِّ وَسَلِّمْ وَبَارِكْ عَلَى سَيِّدِنَا مُحَمَّدٍ وَعَلَى أٰلِهٖ وَ أَزِلْ عَنْ قُلُوْبِنَا بِنُوْرِهِ ظُلْمَةَ الْحِجَابِ .

Wa ṣalli wa sallim wa bārik `alā Sayyidinā Muḥammadin wa `alā Ālihī, Wa azil `an qulūbinā bi Nūrihī ẓulmata ‘l-ḥijāb.

and send prayers upon, salute and bless our Master Muḥammad and his Family and eliminate the veil's darkness from our hearts by his light!

وَصَلِّ وَسَلِّمْ وَبَارِكْ عَلَى سَيِّدِنَا مُحَمَّدٍ وَعَلَى أٰلِهٖ وَأَلْهِمْنَا الْحِكْمَةَ وَالصَّوَابَ .

Wa ṣalli wa sallim wa bārik `alā Sayyidinā Muḥammadin wa `alā Ālihī, Wa al-himnā ‘l-ḥikmata wa ‘ṣ-ṣawāb.

and send prayers upon, salute and bless our Master Muḥammad and his Family and inspire us with wisdom and accuracy!

58

وَصَلِّ وَ سَلِّمْ وَبَارِكْ عَلَى سَيِّدِنَا مُحَمَّدٍ وَعَلَى أٰلِهٖ وَاسْقِنَا مِنْ لَدُنْكَ صَافِيَ الشَّرَابِ .

Wa ṣalli wa sallim wa bārik `alā Sayyidinā Muḥammadin wa `alā Ālihī, Wa ‘sqinā min ladunka ṣāfiya ‘sh-sharāb.

and send prayers upon, salute and bless our Master Muḥammad and his Family and slake us from Your Self with the purest drink!

وَصَلِّ وَسَلِّمْ وَبَارِكْ عَلَى سَيِّدِنَا مُحَمَّدٍ وَعَلَى أٰلِهٖ وَفَهِّمْنَا أَسْرَارَ الْكِتَابِ .

Wa ṣalli wa sallim wa bārik `alā Sayyidinā Muḥammadin wa `alā Ālihī, Wa fahhimnā asrāra ‘l-kitāb.

and send prayers upon, salute and bless our Master Muḥammad and his Family and let us understand the secrets of the book!

وَصَلِّ وَسَلِّمْ وَبَارِكْ عَلَى سَيِّدِنَا مُحَمَّدٍ وَعَلَى أٰلِهٖ وَاجْعَلْنَا بِالصَّلَاةِ عَلَيْهِ مِنَ الْأَنْجَابِ .

Wa ṣalli wa sallim wa bārik `alā Sayyidinā Muḥammadin wa `alā Ālihī, Wa ‘j`alnā bi ‘ṣ-Ṣalāti `alayhi min ‘l-anjāb.

and send prayers upon, salute and bless our Master Muḥammad and his Family and make us, by sending prayers upon him, of the elect!

59

وَصَلِّ وَسَلِّمْ وَبَارِكْ عَلَى سَيِّدِنَا مُحَمَّدٍ وَعَلَى أٰلِهٖ وَأَدْخِلْنَا حَظِيْرَةَ الْقُدْسِ فِيْ جُمْلَةِ الْأَحْبَابِ .

Wa ṣalli wa sallim wa bārik `alā Sayyidinā Muḥammadin wa `alā Ālihī, Wa adkhil nā haẓīrata ‘l-qudsi fī jumlati ‘l-aḥbāb.

and send prayers upon, salute and bless our Master Muḥammad and his Family and enter us in the Sanctuary among the most beloved group!

وَصَلِّ وَسَلِّمْ وَبَارِكْ عَلَى سَيِّدِنَا مُحَمَّدٍ وَعَلَى أٰلِهٖ سَائِرِ الْأَنْبِيَاءِ وَ الْأَصْفِيَاءِ وَ الْأٓلِ وَ الْأَصْحَابِ .

Wa ṣalli wa sallim wa bārik `alā Sayyidinā Muḥammadin wa `alā Ālihī, Wa `alā sā’iri ‘l-Anbiyā’i wa ‘l-Aṣfiyā’i wa ‘l-Āli wa ‘l-Aṣḥāb.

and send prayers upon, salute and bless our Master Muḥammad and all the Prophets, Purified, Folk and Companions!

60

اَللَّهُمَّ صَلِّ وَسَلِّمْ وَبَارِكْ عَلَى سَيِّدِنَا مُحَمَّدٍ وَعَلَى أٰلِهٖ الَّذِيْ جَاءَ بِالْأٓيَاتِ الْبَيِّنَاتِ .

Allāhumma ṣalli wa sallim wa bārik `alā Sayyidinā Muḥammadin wa `alā Ālihī, Alladhī jā’a bi ‘l-āyāti ‘l-bayyināt.

O Allah send prayers upon, salute and bless our Master Muḥammad and his Family the one who came with indisputable verses!

وَصَلِّ وَسَلِّمْ وَبَارِكْ عَلَى سَيِّدِنَا مُحَمَّدٍ وَعَلَى أٰلِهٖ الْمُؤَيَّدِ بِجَلَائِلِ الْمُعْجِزَاتِ .

Wa ṣalli wa sallim wa bārik `alā Sayyidinā Muḥammadin wa `alā Ālihī, al-Mu’ayyadi bi jalā’ili ‘l-mu`jizāt.

and send prayers upon, salute and bless our Master Muḥammad and his Family the supported by immense miracles!

وَصَلِّ وَسَلِّمْ وَبَارِكْ عَلَى سَيِّدِنَا مُحَمَّدٍ وَعَلَى أٰلِهٖ الْقَائِلِ إِنَّمَا الْأَعْمَالُ بِالنِّيَّاتِ .

Wa ṣalli wa sallim wa bārik `alā Sayyidinā Muḥammadin wa `alā Ālihī, al-Qā’ili innama ‘l-a‘mālu bi ‘n-niyyāt.

and send prayers upon, salute and bless our Master Muḥammad and his Family the coiner of “Deeds are but by intention”.

61

وَصَلِّ وَسَلِّمْ وَبَارِكْ عَلَى سَيِّدِنَا مُحَمَّدٍ وَعَلَى أٰلِهٖ السَّارِيْ سِرُّهُ فِيْ سَائِرِ الْكَائِنَاتِ .

Wa ṣalli wa sallim wa bārik `alā Sayyidinā Muḥammadin wa `alā Ālihī, as-Sārī sirruhū fī sā’iri ‘l-kā’ināt.

and send prayers upon, salute and bless our Master Muḥammad and his Family whose secret penetrates the depths of existence!

وَصَلِّ وَسَلِّمْ وَبَارِكْ عَلَى سَيِّدِنَا مُحَمَّدٍ وَعَلَى أٰلِهٖ وَكَفِّرْ بِهَا عَنَّا السَّيِّئَاتِ .

Wa ṣalli wa sallim wa bārik `alā Sayyidinā Muḥammadin wa `alā Ālihī, Wa kaffir bi hā `an na ‘s-sayyi’āt.

and send prayers upon, salute and bless our Master Muḥammad and his Family and thereby hide our faults!

62

وَصَلِّ وَسَلِّمْ وَبَارِكْ عَلَى سَيِّدِنَا مُحَمَّدٍ وَعَلَى أٰلِهٖ وَأَيِّدْنَا بِالْكَرَامَاتِ .

Wa ṣalli wa sallim wa bārik `alā Sayyidinā Muḥammadin wa `alā Ālihī, Wa ayyidnā bi ‘l-karāmāt.

and send prayers upon, salute and bless our Master Muḥammad and his Family and assist us with Miracles.

وَصَلِّ وَسَلِّمْ وَبَارِكْ عَلَى سَيِّدِنَا مُحَمَّدٍ وَعَلَى أٰلِهٖ وَجَمِّلْنَا بِجَمِيْلِ الصِّفَاتِ .

Wa ṣalli wa sallim wa bārik `alā Sayyidinā Muḥammadin wa `alā Ālihī, Wa jammil nā bi jamīli ‘ṣ-ṣifāt.

and send prayers upon, salute and bless our Master Muḥammad and his Family and beautify us with beautiful traits!

وَصَلِّ وَسَلِّمْ وَبَارِكْ عَلَى سَيِّدِنَا مُحَمَّدٍ وَعَلَى أٰلِهٖ وَأَزِلْ عَنْ قُلُوْبِنَا حُبَّ الرِّئَاسَةِ وَجَمِيْعَ الشَّهَوَاتِ .

Wa ṣalli wa sallim wa bārik `alā Sayyidinā Muḥammadin wa `alā Ālihī, Wa azil `an qulūbinā ḥubba ‘r-ri’āsati wa jamī`a ‘sh-shahawāt.

and send prayers upon, salute and bless our Master Muḥammad and his Family and remove from our hearts the love of Mastership and all base desires!

63

وَصَلِّ وَسَلِّمْ وَبَارِكْ عَلَى سَيِّدِنَا مُحَمَّدٍ وَعَلَى أٰلِهٖ وَأَنْعِمْ عَلَيْنَا بِتَجَلِّي الْأَسْمَاءِ وَالصِّفَاتِ .

Wa ṣalli wa sallim wa bārik `alā Sayyidinā Muḥammadin wa `alā Ālihī, Wa an`im `alaynā bi ‘t-tajallī ‘l-Asmā’i wa ‘ṣ-Ṣifāt.

and send prayers upon, salute and bless our Master Muḥammad and his Family and favor us with revealed names and attributes!

وَصَلِّ وَسَلِّمْ وَبَارِكْ عَلَى سَيِّدِنَا مُحَمَّدٍ وَعَلَى أٰلِهٖ وَأَغْرِقْنَا فِيْ عَيْنِ بَحْرِ الْوَحْدَةِ السَّارِيَةِ فِيْ جَمِيْعِ الْمَوْجُوْدَاتِ .

Wa ṣalli wa sallim wa bārik `alā Sayyidinā Muḥammadin wa `alā Ālihī, Wa aghriqnā fī `ayni baḥri ‘l-waḥdati ‘s-sāriyati fī jamī`i ‘l-mawjūdāt.

and send prayers upon, salute and bless our Master Muḥammad and his Family and drown us in the essential sea of penetrating Unity in all that exists!

64

وَصَلِّ وَسَلِّمْ وَبَارِكْ عَلَى سَيِّدِنَا مُحَمَّدٍ وَعَلَى أٰلِهٖ وَأَبْقِنَا بِكَ لَا بِنَا فِيْ جَمِيْعِ اللَّحَظَاتِ .

Wa ṣalli wa sallim wa bārik `alā Sayyidinā Muḥammadin wa `alā Ālihī, wa abqinā bika lā binā fī jamī`i ‘l-laḥaẓāt.

and send prayers upon, salute and bless our Master Muḥammad and his Family and let us remain with You not with us in every instant!

وَصَلِّ وَسَلِّمْ وَبَارِكْ عَلَى سَيِّدِنَا مُحَمَّدٍ وَعَلَى أٰلِهٖ وَانْشُرْ عَلَيْنَا نِعْمَتَكَ الْمَخْصُوْصَةَ بِأَهْلِ الْعِنَايَاتِ .

Wa ṣalli wa sallim wa bārik `alā Sayyidinā Muḥammadin wa `alā Ālihī, Wa ‘nshur ‘alaynā ni`mataka ‘l-makhṣūṣata bi ahli ‘l-`ināyāt.

and send prayers upon, salute and bless our Master Muḥammad and his Family and spread Your special favors upon us by those who care!

65

وَصَلِّ وَسَلِّمْ وَبَارِكْ عَلَى سَيِّدِنَا مُحَمَّدٍ وَعَلَى أٰلِهٖ وَصَحَابَتِهِ وَعَلَى كُلِّ مَنْ صَدَّقَ بِرِسَالَتِهِ وَالْطُفْ بِنَا وَبِوَالِدِيْنَا، وَبِسَائِرِ الْمُسْلِمِيْنَ وَالْمُسْلِمَاتِ، فِي الْحَيَاةِ وَبَعْدَ الْمَمَاتِ .

Wa ṣalli wa sallim wa bārik `alā Sayyidinā Muḥammadin wa `alā Ālihī, Wa Ṣaḥābatihī wa `alā kulli man ṣaddaqa bi risālatihī, Wa ‘lṭuf binā wa bi wālidīnā wa bi sā’iri ‘l-muslimīna wa ‘l-muslimāti, Fī ‘l-ḥayāti wa ba`da ‘l-mamāt.

and send prayers upon, salute and bless our Master Muḥammad and his Family, Companions and everyone who affirms his Messengership and be gentle with us and our parents and the remaining Muslim men and women in this life and after death!

اَللَّهُمَّ صَلِّ وَسَلِّمْ وَبَارِكْ عَلَى سَيِّدِنَا مُحَمَّدٍ وَعَلَى أٰلِهٖ عَدَدَ كُلِّ حَادِثٍ .

Allāhumma ṣalli wa sallim wa bārik `alā Sayyidinā Muḥammadin wa `alā Ālihī, `Adada kulli ḥādith.

O Allah send prayers upon, salute and bless our Master Muḥammad and his Family the number of every occurrence!

66

وَصَلِّ وَسَلِّمْ وَبَارِكْ عَلَى سَيِّدِنَا مُحَمَّدٍ وَعَلَى أٰلِهٖ صَلَاةً يَعُمُّ نُوْرُهَا جَمِيْعَ الْحَوَادِثِ .

Wa ṣalli wa sallim wa bārik `alā Sayyidinā Muḥammadin wa `alā Ālihī, Ṣalātan ya‘ummu nūruhā jamī`a ‘l-ḥawādith.

and send prayers upon, salute and bless our Master Muḥammad and his Family a prayer whose light pervades all occurrences!

وَصَلِّ وَسَلِّمْ وَبَارِكْ عَلَى سَيِّدِنَا مُحَمَّدٍ وَعَلَى أٰلِهٖ مَا صَدَقَ صَادِقٌ وَنَكَثَ نَاكِثٌ .

Wa ṣalli wa sallim wa bārik `alā Sayyidinā Muḥammadin wa `alā Ālihī, Mā ṣadaqa ṣādiqun wa nakatha nākith.

and send prayers upon, salute and bless our Master Muḥammad and his Family all that the truthful confirm and the disloyal violate!

وَصَلِّ وَسَلِّمْ وَبَارِكْ عَلَى سَيِّدِنَا مُحَمَّدٍ وَعَلَى أٰلِهٖ وَاكْفِنَا شَرَّ الْحَوَادِثِ .

Wa ṣalli wa sallim wa bārik `alā Sayyidinā Muḥammadin wa `alā Ālihī, Wa ‘kfinā sharra ‘l-ḥawādith.

70

O Allah send prayers upon, salute and bless our Master Muḥammad and his Family the Best of Creation without exception!

وَصَلِّ وَسَلِّمْ وَبَارِكْ عَلَى سَيِّدِنَا مُحَمَّدٍ وَعَلَى أٰلِهٖ صَلَاةً تُزِيْلُ بِهَا عَنَّا الْوَهْمَ وَالْنِّفَاقَ .

Wa ṣalli wa sallim wa bārik `alā Sayyidinā Muḥammadin wa `alā Ālihī, Ṣalātan tuzīlu bi hā `annā ‘l-wahma wa ‘n-nifāq.

and send prayers upon, salute and bless our Master Muḥammad and his Family a prayer by which delusion and hypocrisy are removed!

92

وَصَلِّ وَسَلِّمْ وَبَارِكْ عَلَى سَيِّدِنَا مُحَمَّدٍ وَعَلَى أٰلِهٖ صَلَاةً تُدْخِلُنَا بِهَا حَضْرَةَ الْإِطْلَاقِ .

Wa ṣalli wa sallim wa bārik `alā Sayyidinā Muḥammadin wa `alā Ālihī, Ṣalātan tudkhilunā bi hā ḥadrata ‘l-itlāq.

and send prayers upon, salute and bless our Master Muḥammad and his Family a prayer driving us into midst of liberation!

وَصَلِّ وَسَلِّمْ وَبَارِكْ عَلَى سَيِّدِنَا مُحَمَّدٍ وَعَلَى أٰلِهٖ أُولِي الْبأْسِ الشَّدِيْدِ عِنْدَ التَّلَاقِ .

Wa ṣalli wa sallim wa bārik `alā Sayyidinā Muḥammadin wa `alā Ālihī, Ulī ‘l-ba’si sh-shadīdi `inda ‘t-talāq.

and send prayers upon, salute and bless our Master Muḥammad and his Family those of intense fortitude in battle!

اَللَّهُمَّ صَلِّ وَ سَلِّمْ وَبَارِكْ عَلَى سَيِّدِنَا مُحَمَّدٍ وَعَلَى أٰلِهٖ مَا تَحَرَّكَتِ الْأَفْلَاكُ .

Allāhumma ṣalli wa sallim wa bārik `alā Sayyidinā Muḥammadin wa `alā Ālihī, Mā taḥarrakati ‘l-aflāk.

O Allah send prayers upon, salute and bless our Master Muḥammad and his Family in all that moves in orbit!

93

وَصَلِّ وَسَلِّمْ وَبَارِكْ عَلَى سَيِّدِنَا مُحَمَّدٍ وَعَلَى أٰلِهٖ عَدَدَ تَسْبِيْحِ الْأَمْلَاكِ .

Wa ṣalli wa sallim wa bārik `alā Sayyidinā Muḥammadin wa `alā Ālihī, `Adada tasbīḥi ‘l-amlāk.

and send prayers upon, salute and bless our Master Muḥammad and his Family the number of Angels' glorification of Allah!

اَللَّهُمَّ صَلِّ وَسَلِّمْ وَبَارِكْ عَلَى سَيِّدِنَا مُحَمَّدٍ وَعَلَى أٰلِهٖ بَطَلِ الْأَبْطَالِ .

Allāhumma ṣalli wa sallim wa bārik `alā Sayyidinā Muḥammadin wa `alā Ālihī, Baṭali ‘l-abṭāl.

O Allah send prayers upon, salute and bless our Master Muḥammad and his Family the most heroic of heroes!

وَصَلِّ وَسَلِّمْ وَ بَارِكْ عَلَى سَيِّدِنَا مُحَمَّدٍ وَعَلَى أٰلِهٖ مَعْدِنِ الْجُوْدِ وَ النَّوَالِ .

Wa ṣalli wa sallim wa bārik `alā Sayyidinā Muḥammadin wa `alā Ālihī, Ma`dini ‘l-jūdi wa ‘n-nawāl.

and send prayers upon, salute and bless our Master Muḥammad and his Family the source of generosity and benefit!.

94

وَصَلِّ وَسَلِّمْ وَبَارِكْ عَلَى سَيِّدِنَا مُحَمَّدٍ وَعَلَى أٰلِهٖ وَ أَذِقْنَا لَذَّةَ الْوِصَالِ .

Wa ṣalli wa sallim wa bārik `alā Sayyidinā Muḥammadin wa `alā Ālihī, Wa adhiqnā ladhdhata ‘l-wiṣāl.

and send prayers upon, salute and bless our Master Muḥammad and his Family and let us taste the pleasure of reunion!

وَصَلِّ وَسَلِّمْ وَبَارِكْ عَلَى سَيِّدِنَا مُحَمَّدٍ وَعَلَى أٰلِهٖ كَمَلَةِ الرِّجَالِ .

Wa ṣalli wa sallim wa bārik `alā Sayyidinā Muḥammadin wa `alā Ālihī, Kamalati ‘r-rijāl.

and send prayers upon, salute and bless our Master Muḥammad and his Family the most complete of men!

اَللَّهُمَّ صَلِّ وَ سَلِّمْ وَبَارِكْ عَلَى سَيِّدِنَا مُحَمَّدٍ وَعَلَى أٰلِهٖ السَّيِّدِ الْهُمَامِ .

Allāhumma ṣalli wa sallim wa bārik `alā Sayyidinā Muḥammadin wa `alā Ālihī, Al-Sayyidi ‘l-humām.

O Allah send prayers upon, salute and bless our Master Muḥammad and his Family the chief of the gallant!

95

وَصَلِّ وَسَلِّمْ وَبَارِكْ عَلَى سَيِّدِنَا مُحَمَّدٍ وَعَلَى أٰلِهٖ أَفْضَلِ الرُّسُلِ الْكِرَامِ عَلَيْهِ وَعَلَيْهِمْ أَفْضَلُ الصَّلَاةِ وَ السَّلَامِ عَلَى مَمَرِّ اللَّيَالِيْ وَالْأَيَّامِ .

Wa ṣalli wa sallim wa bārik `alā Sayyidinā Muḥammadin wa `alā Ālihī, Afḍali ‘r-Rusuli ‘l-Kirāmi `alayhi wa `alayhim afḍalu ‘ṣ-Ṣalāti wa ‘s-Salāmi `alā mamarri ‘l-layālī wa ‘l-ayyām.

and send prayers upon, salute and bless our Master Muḥammad and his Family the best of noble Messengers, upon him and them, the best prayer and peace in the elapsing of nights and days!

وَصَلِّ وَسَلِّمْ وَبَارِكْ عَلَى سَيِّدِنَا مُحَمَّدٍ وَعَلَى أٰلِهٖ صَلَاةً تُنَجِّيْنَا بِهَا مِنَ الشُّكُوْكِ وَ الْأَوْهَامِ .

Wa ṣalli wa sallim wa bārik `alā Sayyidinā Muḥammadin wa `alā Ālihī, Ṣalātan tunajjīnā bi hā mina sh-shukūki wa ‘l-awhām.

and send prayers upon, salute and bless our Master Muḥammad and his Family a prayer saving us from doubts and delusions!

وَصَلِّ وَسَلِّمْ وَبَارِكْ عَلَى سَيِّدِنَا مُحَمَّدٍ وَعَلَى أٰلِهٖ الْأَئِمَّةِ الْأَعْلَامِ .

Wa ṣalli wa sallim wa bārik `alā Sayyidinā Muḥammadin wa `alā Ālihī, Al-A’immati ‘l-a`lām.

and send prayers upon, salute and bless our Master Muḥammad and his Family the distinguished Masters!

96

اَللَّهُمَّ صَلِّ وَ سَلِّمْ وَبَارِكْ عَلَى سَيِّدِنَا مُحَمَّدٍ وَعَلَى أٰلِهٖ سَيِّدِ الْأَكْوَانِ .

Allāhumma ṣalli wa sallim wa bārik `alā Sayyidinā Muḥammadin wa `alā Ālihī, Sayyidi ‘l-akwān.

O Allah send prayers upon, salute and bless our Master Muḥammad and his Family, the chief of all existence!

وَصَلِّ وَسَلِّمْ وَبَارِكْ عَلَى سَيِّدِنَا مُحَمَّدٍ وَعَلَى أٰلِهٖ صَلَاةً تَمْلَأُ الْأَمْكِنَةَ وَ الْأَزْمَانَ .

Wa ṣalli wa sallim wa bārik `alā Sayyidinā Muḥammadin wa `alā Ālihī, Ṣalātan tamla’u ‘l-amkinata wa ‘l-azmān.

and send prayers upon, salute and bless our Master Muḥammad and his Family a prayer filling all places and times!

وَصَلِّ وَسَلِّمْ وَبَارِكْ عَلَى سَيِّدِنَا مُحَمَّدٍ وَعَلَى أٰلِهٖ صَلَاةً نَرْتَقِيْ بِهَا إِلَى مَقَامِ الْمَعْرِفَةِ وَالْإِحْسَانِ .

Wa ṣalli wa sallim wa bārik `alā Sayyidinā Muḥammadin wa `alā Ālihī, Ṣalātan nartaqī bi hā ilā maqāmi ‘l-ma`rifati wa ‘l-ihsān.

and send prayers upon, salute and bless our Master Muḥammad and his Family, a prayer raising us to the rank of realization and perfection!

97

وَصَلِّ وَسَلِّمْ وَبَارِكْ عَلَى سَيِّدِنَا مُحَمَّدٍ وَعَلَى أٰلِهٖ الْأَئِمَّةِ الْأَعْيَانِ .

Wa ṣalli wa sallim wa bārik `alā Sayyidinā Muḥammadin wa `alā Ālihī, Al-A’immati ‘l-a`yān.

and send prayers upon, salute and bless our Master Muḥammad and his Family the eminent leaders!

اَللَّهُمَّ صَلِّ وَ سَلِّمْ وَبَارِكْ عَلَى سَيِّدِنَا مُحَمَّدٍ وَعَلَى أٰلِهٖ الْعَالِي الْقَدْرِ الْعَظِيْمِ الْجَاهِ .

Allāhumma ṣalli wa sallim wa bārik `alā Sayyidinā Muḥammadin wa `alā Ālihī, Al-Āli ‘l-qadri ‘l-aẓīmi ‘l-jāh.

O Allah send prayers upon, salute and bless our Master Muḥammad and his Family of Outstanding Destiny of Great Dignity!

وَصَلِّ وَسَلِّمْ وَبَارِكْ عَلَى سَيِّدِنَا مُحَمَّدٍ وَعَلَى أٰلِهٖ وَ أَطْلِعْنَا عَلَى أَسْرَارِ لَآ إِلٰهَ اِلَّا اللّٰهُ .

Wa ṣalli wa sallim wa bārik `alā Sayyidinā Muḥammadin wa `alā Ālihī, Wa aṭli`nā `alā asrāri lā ilāha illa ‘lLāh.

and send prayers upon, salute and bless our Master Muḥammad and his Family and disclose to us the secret of "La ilaha illa Allah!”

98

اَللَّهُمَّ صَلِّ وَ سَلِّمْ وَبَارِكْ عَلَى سَيِّدِنَا مُحَمَّدٍ وَعَلَى أٰلِهٖ الَّذِيْ مَا نَطَقَ عَنِ الْهَوَى .

Allāhumma ṣalli wa sallim wa bārik `alā Sayyidinā Muḥammadin wa `alā Ālihī, Alladhī mā naṭaqa `ani ‘l-hawā.

O Allah send prayers upon, salute and bless our Master Muḥammad and his Family who never spoke from his own desire!

وَصَلِّ وَسَلِّمْ وَبَارِكْ عَلَى سَيِّدِنَا مُحَمَّدٍ وَعَلَى أٰلِهٖ الَّذِيْ مَا ضَلَّ عَنِ الْحَقِّ وَمَا غَوَى .

Wa ṣalli wa sallim wa bārik `alā Sayyidinā Muḥammadin wa `alā Ālihī, Alladhī mā ḍalla `ani ‘l-haqqi wa mā ghawā.

and send prayers upon, salute and bless our Master Muḥammad and his Family who never strayed from truth nor deviated!

وَصَلِّ وَسَلِّمْ وَبَارِكْ عَلَى سَيِّدِنَا مُحَمَّدٍ وَعَلَى أٰلِهٖ وَأَلْبِسْنَا بِالصَّلَاةِ عَلَيْهِ لِبَاسَ التَّقْوَى .

Wa ṣalli wa sallim wa bārik `alā Sayyidinā Muḥammadin wa `alā Ālihī, wa albisnā bi ‘ṣ-Ṣalāti `alayhi libāsa ‘t-taqwā.

and send prayers upon, salute and bless our Master Muḥammad and his Family and clothe us by sending prayers upon him the garments of Allah-consciousness!

99

وَصَلِّ وَسَلِّمْ وَبَارِكْ عَلَى سَيِّدِنَا مُحَمَّدٍ وَعَلَى أٰلِهٖ الَّذِيْ مَا ضَلَّ عَنِ الْحَقِّ وَمَا غَوَى .

Wa ṣalli wa sallim wa bārik `alā Sayyidinā Muḥammadin wa `alā Ālihī, Alladhī mā ḍalla `ani ‘l-haqqi wa mā ghawā.

and send prayers upon, salute and bless our Master Muḥammad and his Family who never strayed from truth nor deviated!

وَصَلِّ وَسَلِّمْ وَبَارِكْ عَلَى سَيِّدِنَا مُحَمَّدٍ وَعَلَى أٰلِهٖ وَأَلْبِسْنَا بِالصَّلَاةِ عَلَيْهِ لِبَاسَ التَّقْوَى .

Wa ṣalli wa sallim wa bārik `alā Sayyidinā Muḥammadin wa `alā Ālihī, wa albisnā bi ‘ṣ-Ṣalāti `alayhi libāsa ‘t-taqwā.

and send prayers upon, salute and bless our Master Muḥammad and his Family and clothe us by sending prayers upon him the garments of Allah-consciousness!

وَصَلِّ وَسَلِّمْ وَبَارِكْ عَلَى سَيِّدِنَا مُحَمَّدٍ وَعَلَى أٰلِهٖ وَطَهِّرْنَا بِهَا مِنَ الشَّكْوَى وَالدَّعْوَى .

Wa ṣalli wa sallim wa bārik `alā Sayyidinā Muḥammadin wa `alā Ālihī, Wa tahhirnā bi hā mina sh-shakwā wa ‘d-da`wā.

and send prayers upon, salute and bless our Master Muḥammad and his Family and purify us thereby from complaint and pretension!

100

وَصَلِّ وَسَلِّمْ وَبَارِكْ عَلَى سَيِّدِنَا مُحَمَّدٍ وَعَلَى أٰلِهٖ وَ كُفَّ بِهَا عَنَّا الْأَسْوَاءَ وَالْبَلْوَى . [وَ الْأَمْرَاضِ]

Wa ṣalli wa sallim wa bārik `alā Sayyidinā Muḥammadin wa `alā Ālihī, Wa kuffa bi hā `annā ‘l-aswā’a wa ‘l-balwā [wa ‘l-amrāḍ].

and send prayers upon, salute and bless our Master Muḥammad and his Family and avert from us thereby all trial and tribulation [and sickness]!

وَصَلِّ وَسَلِّمْ وَبَارِكْ عَلَى سَيِّدِنَا مُحَمَّدٍ وَعَلَى أٰلِهٖ وَ الْطُفْ بِنَا بِبَرَكَاتِهَا فِي السِّرِّ وَ النَّجْوَى .

Wa ṣalli wa sallim wa bārik `alā Sayyidinā Muḥammadin wa `alā Ālihī, Wa ‘lṭuf bi nā bi barakātihā fi ‘s-sirri wa ‘n-najwā.

and send prayers upon, salute and bless our Master Muḥammad and his Family and be kind to us thereby secretly and in secret council!

اَللَّهُمَّ صَلِّ وَ سَلِّمْ وَبَارِكْ عَلَى سَيِّدِنَا مُحَمَّدٍ وَعَلَى أٰلِهٖ ذَوِي الْمَقَامِ الْأَعْلَى وَ السِّرِّ الْأَجْلَى .

Allāhumma ṣalli wa sallim wa bārik `alā Sayyidinā Muḥammadin wa `alā Ālihī, Dha wī ‘l-maqāmi ‘l-a`lā wa ‘s-sirri ‘l-ajlā.

O Allah send prayers upon, salute and bless our Master Muḥammad and his Family those of highest ranks and the most sublime secret!

101

وَصَلِّ وَسَلِّمْ وَبَارِكْ عَلَى سَيِّدِنَا مُحَمَّدٍ وَعَلَى أٰلِهٖ فِي الْخَلَا وَالْمَلَا .

Wa ṣalli wa sallim wa bārik `alā Sayyidinā Muḥammadin wa `alā Ālihī, Fi ‘l-khalā wa ‘l-malā.

and send prayers upon, salute and bless our Master Muḥammad and his Family, in solitude and with company!

وَصَلِّ وَسَلِّمْ وَبَارِكْ عَلَى سَيِّدِنَا مُحَمَّدٍ وَعَلَى أٰلِهٖ سَادَاتِ أَهْلِ الْعُلَا .

Wa ṣalli wa sallim wa bārik `alā Sayyidinā Muḥammadin wa `alā Ālihī, Sādāti ahli ‘l-`ulā.

and send prayers upon, salute and bless our Master Muḥammad and his Family, leaders of the highest party!

وَصَلِّ وَسَلِّمْ وَبَارِكْ عَلَى سَيِّدِنَا مُحَمَّدٍ وَعَلَى أٰلِهٖ وَ اكْشِفْ لَنَا عَنْ مَقَامَاتِ الْوَلَاءِ وَ الْاِسْتِجْلَاءِ .

Wa ṣalli wa sallim wa bārik `alā Sayyidinā Muḥammadin wa `alā Ālihī, Wa ‘kshif lanā `an maqāmāti ‘l-walā i wa ‘l-istijlā.

and send prayers upon, salute and bless our Master Muḥammad and his Family and unveil us to the processions of allegiance and elucidation!

102

اَللَّهُمَّ صَلِّ وَ سَلِّمْ وَبَارِكْ عَلَى سَيِّدِنَا مُحَمَّدٍ وَعَلَى أٰلِهٖ وَكُلِّ نَبِيٍّ .

Allāhumma ṣalli wa sallim wa bārik `alā Sayyidinā Muḥammadin wa `alā Ālihī, Wa kulli Nabiyy.

O Allah send prayers upon, salute and bless our Master Muḥammad and his Family and every Prophet!

وَصَلِّ وَسَلِّمْ وَبَارِكْ عَلَى سَيِّدِنَا مُحَمَّدٍ وَعَلَى أٰلِهٖ وَكُلِّ مَلَكٍ وَوَلِيٍّ .

Wa ṣalli wa sallim wa bārik `alā Sayyidinā Muḥammadin wa `alā Ālihī wa kulli Malakin wa Waliyy.

and send prayers upon, salute and bless our Master Muḥammad and his Family and every Angel and Saint!

وَصَلِّ وَسَلِّمْ وَبَارِكْ عَلَى سَيِّدِنَا مُحَمَّدٍ وَعَلَى أٰلِهٖ وَكُلِّ عَالِمٍ وَتَقِيٍّ .

Wa ṣalli wa sallim wa bārik `alā Sayyidinā Muḥammadin wa `alā Ālihī wa kulli `ālimin wa taqiyy.

and send prayers upon, salute and bless our Master Muḥammad and his Family and every scholar and pious person!

103

وَصَلِّ وَسَلِّمْ وَبَارِكْ عَلَى سَيِّدِنَا مُحَمَّدٍ وَعَلَى أٰلِهٖ وَأَصْحَابِهِ وَأَتْبَاعِهِ وَ عَلَى سَائِرِ الْمُؤْمِنِيْنَ وَالْمُؤمِنَاتِ ، الْأَحْيَاءِ مِنْهُمْ وَالْأَمْوَاتِ ، وَتَابِعْ بَيْنَنَا وَبَيْنَهُمْ بِالْخَيْرَاتِ وَالْبَرَكَاتِ ، إِنَّكَ قَرِيْبٌ مُجِيْبُ الدَّعَوَاتِ يَا رَبَّ الْعَالَمِيْنَ .

Wa ṣalli wa sallim wa bārik `alā Sayyidinā Muḥammadin wa `alā Ālihī, Wa aṣḥābihī wa atbā`ihī wa `ala sā’iri ‘l-mu’minīna wa ‘l-mu’mināti ‘l-aḥyā’i minhum wa ‘l-amwāti, Wa tābi` baynanā wa baynahum bi ‘l-khayrāti wa ‘l-barakāti, Innaka qarībun mujību ‘d-da`awāti, Yā Rabba ‘l-Ālamīn.

and send prayers upon, salute and bless our Master Muḥammad and his Family, Companions and Followers and the rest of the believing men and women, the living of them and the dead, and follow up with goodness and blessings between us and them, surely You are Near and Responsive to Supplicants, O Lord of the Worlds!
