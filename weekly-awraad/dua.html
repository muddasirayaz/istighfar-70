FINAL DUAH

127

9. Followed by this prayer (ẓikr): [7 times]

اَللَّهُمَّ اغْفِرْ لِيْ وَلِوَالِدَيَّ وَلِجَمِيْعِ الْمُؤْمِنِيْنَ وَ الْمُؤْمِنَاتِ وَ الْمُسْلِمِيْنَ وَ الْمُسْلِمَاتِ الْأَحْيَاءِ مِنْهُمْ وَ الْأَمْوَاتِ ـ سَبْعًا

Allāhumma ‘ghfir lī wa li wālidayya wa li jamī`i ‘l-mu’minīna wa ‘l-mu’mināti, wa ‘l-muslimīna wa ‘l-muslimāti, al-aḥyā’i min hum wa ‘l-amwāt. (7 times).

O Allah, forgive me, my parents, all the believers and all the Muslims, men and women! Living or dead.

10. Followed by this prayer (ẓikr): [7 times]

اَللَّهُمَّ افْعَلْ بِيْ وَبِهِمْ عَاجِلًا وَ آجِلًا فِي الدِّيْنِ وَالدُّنْيَا وَالْآخِرَةِ مَا أَنْتَ لَهُ أَهْلٌ، وَلَا تَفْعَلْ بِنَا يَا مَوْلَانَا مَا نَحْنُ لَهُ أَهْلٌ، إِنَّكَ غَفُوْرٌ حَلِيْمٌ جَوَادٌ كَرِيْمٌ رَؤُوْفٌ رَحِيْمٌ ـ سَبْعًا

Allāhumma ‘f`al bī wa bi him `ājilan wa ājilan fī ‘d-Dīni wa ‘d-Dunyā wa ‘l-Ākhirati mā anta la hū ahl, wa lā taf`al bi nā Yā Mawlānā mā naḥnu la hū ahl, inna ka Ghafūrun Ḥalīmun Jawādun Karīmun Ra’ūfun Raḥīm.

O Allah, do for me and them, now and in the future, in this life and the hereafter, what You are deserving of; do not do with us, O our Master, what we are deserving of, surely You are Forgiving, Forebearing, Magnanimous, Generous, Loving and Especially Merciful!

128

بِسْمِ اللّٰهِ الرَّحْمٰنِ الرَّحِيْمِ -

Bismi ‘lLāhi ‘r-Raḥmāni ‘r-Raḥīm.

In the name of Allah, The Profoundly Merciful, The Continually Merciful.

رَبِّ أَعُوْذُ بِكَ مِنْ هَمَزَاتِ الشَّيَاطِيْنِۙ ۝۹۷ وَ أَعُوْذُ بِكَ رَبِّ أَنْ يَّحْضُرُوْنِ ۝۹۸

Rabbi a`ūdhu bi ka min hamazāti ‘sh-Shayātīni, Wa a`ūdhu bi ka Rabbi a ‘yyaḥḍurūn.

My Lord, I seek refuge with You from the temptations of devils, I seek refuge in You, my Lord, lest they appear!

SuraMu’minun Verse97,98

اَللَّهُمَّ إِنِّيْ أَعُوْذُ بِكَ مِنَ الْهَمِّ وَالْحَزَنِ، وَ أَعُوْذُ بِكَ مِنَ الْعَجْزِ وَالْكَسَلِ، وَ أَعُوْذُ بِكَ مِنَ الْجُبْنِ وَالْبُخْلِ، وَ أَعُوْذُ بِكَ مِنْ غَلَبَةِ الدَّيْنِ وَقَهْرِ الرِّجَالِ ـ ثلاثا

Allāhumma innī a`ūdhu bi ka min ‘l-hammi wa ‘l-ḥazani, Wa a`ūdhu bi ka min ‘l-`ajzi wa ‘l-kasali, Wa a`ūdhu bi ka min ‘l-jubni wa ‘l-bukhli, Wa a`ūdhu bi ka min ghalabati ‘d-dayni wa qahri ‘r-rijāl.

O Allah, I surely seek refuge with You from sorrow and sadness. I seek refuge with You from incapacity and laziness. I seek refuge with You from cowardice and stinginess. I seek refuge with You from hopelessness of debt and subjugation by men. [3 times]

اَللَّهُمَّ إِنِّيْ أَعُوْذُ بِكَ مِنَ الْفَقْرِ وَالْعَيْلَةِ، وَأَعُوْذُ بِكَ مِنْ كُلِّ بَلِيَّةٍ.

Allāhumma innī a`ūdhu bi ka mina ‘l-faqri wa ‘l-`aylati, Wa a`ūdhu bi ka min kulli baliyyattin.

O Allah, I surely seek refuge with You from need and poverty. I seek refuge with You from every tribulation.

129

اَللَّهُمَّ إِنِّيْ أَعُوْذُ بِكَ مِنَ الْفَقْرِ إِلَّا إِلَيْكَ ، وَ مِنَ الذُّلِّ إِلَّا لَكَ، وَمِنَ الْخَوْفِ إِلَّا مِنْكَ ، وَ أَعُوْذُ بِكَ أَنْ أَقُوْلَ زُوْرًا أَوْ أَغْشَى فُجُوْرًا أَوْ أَكُوْنَ بِكَ مَغْرُوْرًا .

Allāhumma innī a`ūdhu bi ka mina ‘l-faqri illā ilayka, Wa mina ‘dh-dhulli illā laka, Wa min ‘l-khawfi illa min ka, Wa a`ūdhu bi ka an aqūla zūran aw aghshā fujūran aw akūna bi ka maghrūrā.

O Allah, I surely seek refuge with you from need except for You, from humility except for You, and from fear except of You! I seek refuge with You lest I speak falsehood or commit immorality or be deluded about You!

اَللَّهُمَّ إِنِّيْ أَعُوْذُ بِكَ مِنَ الْفِتَنِ مَا ظَهَرَ مِنْهَا وَمَا بَطَنَ - ثلاثا

Allāhumma innī a`ūdhu bi ka min ‘l-fitani mā ẓahara min hā wa mā baṭan.

O Allah, I surely seek refuge with you from trials, the apparent of them and the hidden! [3 times]

أَعُوْذُ بِكَلِمَاتِ اللّٰهِ التَّآمَّاتِ مِنْ شَرِّ مَا خَلَقَ ـ ثلاثا

A`ūdhu bi kalimāti ‘lLāhi ‘t-tāmmāti min sharri mā khalaq.

I seek refuge with the Complete Words of Allah from evil of what He created. [3 times]

130

اَللَّهُمَّ إِنِّيْ أَعُوْذُ بِكَ مِنْ أَنْ أَظْلِمَ أَوْ أُظْلَمَ، أَوْ أَبْغِيَ أَوْ يُبْغَى عَلَيَّ ، اَوْ أَطْغَى أَوْ يُطْغَى عَلَيَّ .

Allāhumma innī a`ūdhu bi ka min an aẓlima, aw uẓlama, aw abghiya aw yubghā `alayya, aw aṭghā aw yuṭghā `alayya.

O Allah, I surely seek refuge with you lest I oppress or get oppressed, infringe or am infringed upon, or terrorise or am terrorised!

اَللَّهُمَّ إِنِّيْ أَعُوْذُ بِكَ مِنَ الشَّكِّ وَالشِّرْكِ الظَّاهِرِ وَالْخَفِيِّ، وَالظُّلْمِ وَالْجَوْرِ مِنِّي وَعَلَيَّ .

Allāhumma innī a`ūdhu bi ka min ‘sh-shakki wa ‘sh-shirki ‘ẓ-ẓāhiri wa ‘l-khafiyyi, Wa ẓ-ẓulmi wa ‘l-jawri minnī wa `alayya.

O Allah, I surely seek refuge with you from doubt, polytheism apparent or latent, oppression, and injustice from me or to me!

اَللَّهُمَّ اجْعَلْنِيْ مِنْكَ فِيْ عِيَاذٍ مَنِيْعٍ وَحِرْزٍ حَصِيْنٍ مِنْ جَمِيْعِ خَلْقِكَ، حَتَّى تُبَلِّغَنِيْ أَجَلِيْ مُعَافًى مِنْ كُلِّ بَلِيَّةٍ فِيْ دِيْنِيْ ، وَدُنْيَايَ ، وَبَدَنِيْ ، وَ أَهْلِيْ، وَأَصْحَابِيْ ، وَأَحْبَابِيْ ، يَا رَبَّ الْعَالَمِيْنَ .

Allāhumma ‘j`alnī min ka fī `iyādhin manī`in wa ḥirzin ḥaṣīnin min jamī`i khalqika, Ḥattā tuballighanī ajalī mu`āfan min kulli baliyyatin fī Dīnī, Wa Dunyāya, Wa badanī, wa ahlī, Wa aṣhābī, Wa aḥbābī, Yā Rabba ‘l-Ālamīn.

O Allah, place me, from You in an impenetrable refuge and protective fortress from all of Your creation, until my time comes, exempted from every distress in my religion, worldly life, body, family, companions and loved ones, O Lord of the Worlds!

131

اَللَّهُمَّ اجْعَلْ خَيْرَ أَعْمَالِنَا خَوَاتِيْمَهَا ، وَخَيْرَ أَيَّامِنَا يَوْمَ لِقَائِكَ ،

Allāhumma ‘j`al khayra a`mālinā khwātīmahā, Wa khayra ayyāminā yawma liqā’ika,

O Allah, make the best of our works their seal and the best of our days the Day we meet You!

رَبَّنَآ أَتْمِمْ لَنَا نُوْرَنَا وَ اغْفِرْ لَنَا ۚ إِنَّكَ عَلٰى كُلِّ شَيْءٍ قَدِيْرٌ ۝۸

Rabbanā atmim lanā nūra nā wa ‘ghfir lanā, Inna ka `alā kulli shay’in Qadīr.

Our Lord, complete our light for us and forgive us, surely You are Capable of all things!

Surah Al Tahrim Verse 8

رَبَّنَآ اٰمَنَّا بِمَآ اَنْزَلْتَ وَ اتَّبَعْنَا الرَّسُوْلَ فَاكْتُبْنَا مَعَ الشّٰهِدِيْنَ ۝۵۳

Rabbanā āmannā bi mā anzalta wa ‘t-taba`nā ‘r-Rasūla fa ‘ktubnā ma`a ‘sh-Shāhidīn,

Our Lord, we believe in what You sent and followed the Messenger, so write us among the witnesses!

Sura Al Imran Verse 53

اَللَّهُمَّ اغْفِرْ لَنَا مَا قَدَّمْنَا وَ مَا أَخَّرْنَا وَ مَا أَسْرَرْنَا وَ مَا أَعْلَنَّا وَ مَا أَنْتَ أَعْلَمُ بِهِ مِنَّا .

Allāhumma ‘ghfir lanā mā qaddamnā wa mā akhkharnā wa mā asrarnā wa mā a`lannā wa mā anta a`lamu bi hī minnā.

O Allah, forgive us what we sent forth, what we left behind, what we hid, what we showed and whatever You know of us!

132

اَللَّهُمَّ أَرِنَا الْحَقَّ حَقًّا فَنَتَّبِعَهُ ، وَ أَرِنَا الْبَاطِلَ بَاطِلًا فَنَجْتَنِبَهُ ، بِرَحْمَتِكَ يَاأَرْحَمَ الرَّاحِمِيْنَ .

Allāhumma arinā ‘l-ḥaqqa ḥaqqan fa nattabi`ahū, Wa arinā al-bāṭila bāṭilan fa najtanibahū, bi raḥmatika Yā Arḥama ‘r-Rāhimīn.

O Allah, show us truth as such, so we may follow it, and show us falsehood as such so we can avoid it, by Your Mercy, O Merciful of The Profoundly Merciful!

اَللَّهُمَّ اكْفِنَا بِحَلَالِكَ عَنْ حَرَامِكَ ، وَ أَغْنِنَا بِفَضْلِكَ عَمَّنْ سِوَاكَ .

Allāhumma ‘kfinā bi ḥalālika `an ḥarāmika, Wa aghninā bi faḍlika `amman siwāka,

O Allah, suffice us with Your lawful things rather than Your unlawful and enrich us by Your bounty from asking from others!

اَللَّهُمَّ يَسِّرْ لَنَا أُمُوْرَنَا مَعَ الرَّاحَةِ لِقُلُوْبِنَا وَ أَبْدَانِنَا ، وَالسَّلَامَةِ وَ الْعَافِيَةِ فِيْ دِيْنِنَا وَ دُنْيَانَا وَ آخِرَتِنَا إِنَّكَ عَلٰى كُلِّ شَيْءٍ قَدِيْرٌ .

Allāhumma yassir lanā umūranā ma`a ‘r-rāḥati li qulūbinā wa abdāninā, Wa ‘s-salāmati wa ‘l-`āfiyati fī Dīninā wa Dunyānā wa Ākhiratinā, Inna ka `alā kulli shay’in Qadīr.

O Allah, ease our affairs with rest for our hearts and bodies, security and health in our religion, worldy lives and afterlives, surely You are Capable of all things!

133

اَللَّهُمَّ ارْزُقْنَا حُسْنَ التَّوَكُّلِ عَلَيْكَ ، وَدَوَامَ الْإِقْبَالِ عَلَيْكَ ، وَاكْفِنَا شَرَّ وَسَاوِسِ الشَّيْطَانِ ، وَ قِنَا شَرَّ الْإِنْسِ وَالْجَانِّ ، وَاخْلَعْ عَلَيْنَا خِلَعَ الرِّضْوَانِ ، وَ هَبْ لَنَا حَقِيْقَةَ الْإِيْمَانِ ، وَ تَوَلَّ قَبْضَ أَرْوَاحِنَا عِنْدَ الْأَجَلِ بِيَدِكَ مَعَ شِدَّةِ الشَّوْقِ إِلَى لِقَائِكَ يَارَحْمَنُ .

Allāhumma ‘rzuqnā ḥusna ‘t-tawakkuli `alayka, Wa dawāma ‘l-iqbāli `alayka, Wa ‘kfinā sharra wasāwisi ‘sh-shayṭāni, Wa qinā sharra ‘l-insi wa ‘l-jānni, Wa ‘khla` `alaynā khila`a ‘r-ridwāni, Wa hab lanā ḥaqīqata ‘l-īmāni, Wa tawalla qabḍa arwāḥinā `inda ‘l-ajali bi Yadika ma`a shiddati ‘sh-shawqi ilā liqā’ika Yā Raḥmān.

O Allah, provide us the best of reliance upon You, perpetual nearness and response to You, avail us of satan's evil suggestions, save us from the evils of man and sprite, confer upon us the honorable robe of pleasure, bestow upon us the reality of faith, take possession of our souls at their appointed time Yourself, with intense longing to meet You, O Merciful!

اَللَّهُمَّ إِنَّا نَسْأَلُكَ عِلْمًا نَافِعًا ، وَقَلْبًا خَاشِعًا ، وَنُوْرًا سَاطِعًا ، وَ رِزْقًا وَاسِعًا ، وَشِفَاءً مِنْ كُلِّ دَاءٍ ، وَنَسْأَلُكَ الْغِنَى عَنِ النَّاسِ .

Allāhumma innā nas’alu ka `ilman nāfi`an, Wa qalban khāshi`an, Wa nūran sāṭi`an, Wa rizqan wāsi`an, Wa shifā’an min kulli dā’in, Wa nas’alu ka ‘l-ghinā `ani ‘n-Nās.

O Allah, surely we ask You for beneficial knowledge, humble hearts, brilliant light, vast provision and remedy from every ailment and we ask You from sufficiency from people.

134

رَبِّ اشْرَحْ لِيْ صَدْرِيْۙ ۝۲۵ وَ يَسِّرْ لِيٓ أَمْرِيْۙ ۝۲۶ وَاحْلُلْ عُقْدَةً مِّنْ لِّسَانِيْۙ ۝۲۷ يَفْقَهُوْا قَوْلِيْۖ ۝۲۸

Rabbi ‘shraḥ lī ṣadrī wa yassir lī amrī, waḥlu ‘l `uqdatam ‘min lisānī yafqahū qawlī.

My Lord, open my breast for me, facilitate my task for me and clear the knot from my tongue so others can understand my speech!

Sura Taha Verse 25-28

رَبِّ أَوْزِعْنِيٓ اَنْ اَشْكُرَ نِعْمَتَكَ الَّتِيٓ اَنْعَمْتَ عَلَيَّ وَعَلٰى وَالِدَيَّ وَاَنْ اَعْمَلَ صَالِحًا تَرْضٰهُ وَ أَدْخِلْنِيْ بِرَحْمَتِكَ فِيْ عِبَادِكَ الصّٰلِحِيْنَ ۝۱۹

Rabbi awzi`nī `an ashkura ni`mata ka ‘l-latī an`amta `alayya wa `alā wālidayya, wa an a`mala ṣāliḥan tarḍāhu wa adkhilnī bi raḥmati ka fī `ibādi ka ‘ṣ-Ṣālihīna.

My Lord, inspire me to thank You for Your bounties with which You blessed me and my parents, so I work righteousness with which You are pleased, and join me, by Your mercy, with Your righteous servants!

Sura Al Naml Verse 19

رَبِّ اغْفِرْ وَ ارْحَمْ وَ أَنْتَ خَيْرُ الرّٰحِمِيْنَ ۝۱۱۸

Rabbi ‘ghfir wa ‘rḥam wa anta khayru ‘r-Rāhimīna.

My Lord, forgive and have mercy, You are the Best of The Profoundly Merciful.

Sura Mu’minun Verse 118

135

سُبْحٰنَ رَبِّكَ رَبِّ الْعِزَّةِ عَمَّا يَصِفُوْنَۚ ۝۱۸۰ وَ سَلٰمٌ عَلَى الْمُرْسَلِيْنَۚ ۝۱۸۱ وَ الْحَمْدُ لِلّٰهِ رَبِّ الْعٰلَمِيْنَ ۝۱۸۲

Subhāna Rabbi ka Rabbi ‘l-`Izzati ammā yaṣifūna, wa salāmun `alā ‘l-Mursalīna wa ‘l-ḥamdu li-’lLāhi Rabbi ‘l-`Ālamīn.

Glory to your Lord, Lord of Honor, beyond what they attribute, Peace be upon the Messengers, and Praise be to Allah, Lord of the Worlds!

Sura Saffaat Verse 180-182

اَللَّهُمَّ إِنِّيْ أَسْأَلُكَ لِيْ وَلَهُمْ مِنْ كُلِّ خَيْرٍ سَأَلَكَ مِنْهُ سَيِّدُنَا مُحَمَّدٌ صَلَّى اللّٰهُ عَلَيْهِ وَعَلَى أٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّمَ نَبِيُّكَ وَرَسُوْلُكَ ، وَ أَعُوْذُبِكَ اللَّهُمَّ مِنْ كُلِّ شَرٍّ اسْتَعَاذَكَ مِنْهُ سَيِّدُنَا مُحَمَّدٌ صَلَّى اللّٰهُ عَلَيْهِ وَ عَلَى أٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّمَ نَبِيُّكَ وَ رَسُوْلُكَ .

Allāhumma innī as’alu ka lī wa lahum min kulli khayrin sa’ala ka min hu Sayyidunā Muḥammadun ṣallā ‘llāhu `alayhi wa `alā Ālihī wa sallama Nabiyyuka wa Rasūluka, Wa a`ūdhu bi ka ‘lLāhumma min kulli sharrini ‘sta`ādha ka min hu Sayyidunā Muḥammadun ṣallā ‘llāhu `alayhi wa `alā Ālihī wa sallama Nabiyyuka wa Rasūluk.

O Allah, I surely ask You, for both me and them, every good our Master Muḥammad asked from You, Allah's prayers and peace be upon him and his Family, Your Prophet and Your Messenger! And I seek refuge with You, O Allah, from every evil our Master Muḥammad sought refuge with You from, Allah's prayers and peace be upon him, Your Prophet and Your Messenger.

136

لَآ إِلٰهَ إِلَّا اللّٰهُ مُحَمَّدٌ رَسُوْلُ اللّٰهِ حَقًّا وَصِدْقًا ، وَ صَلِّ وَسَلِّمْ وَبَارِكْ عَلَى جَمِيْعِ الْأَنْبِيَاءِ وَ الْمُرْسَلِيْنَ وَالْحَمْدُ لِلّٰهِ رَبِّ الْعٰلَمِيْنَ .

Lā ilāha illa ‘lLāhu Muḥammadu ‘r-Rasūlu ‘lLāhi ḥaqqan wa ṣidqan, Wa ṣalli wa sallim `alā jamī`i ‘l-Anbiyā’i wa ‘l-Mursalīna wa ‘l-ḥamdu li ‘lLāhi Rabbi ‘l-`Ālamīn.

There is no god but Allah, Muḥammad is the Messenger of Allah, really and truly, and prayers, salutations and blessings be upon all the Prophets and Messengers, and praise Allah, Lord of the Worlds!

اَللَّهُمَّ أَبْلِغْ رُوْحَ سَيِّدِنَا مُحَمَّدٍ صَلَّى اللّٰهُ عَلَيْهِ وَعَلَى أٰلِهٖ مِنِّيْ تَحِيَّةً وَ سَلَامًا – ثلاثا

Allāhumma abligh rūḥa Sayyidinā Muḥammadin ṣallā ‘llāhu `alayhi wa `alā Ālihī minnī taḥiyyatan wa salāmā

O Allah, convey greetings and salutations to the soul of Muḥammad, Allah's prayers be upon him and his Family, from me! [3 times]

137

اَللَّهُمَّ صَلِّ وَسَلِّمْ وَبَارِكْ عَلَى سَيِّدِنَا مُحَمَّدٍ وَعَلَى أٰلِهٖ فِي الْأَوَّلِيْنَ ، وَصَلِّ وَسَلِّمْ وَبَارِكْ عَلَى سَيِّدِنَا مُحَمَّدٍ وَعَلَى أٰلِهٖ فِي الْأٓخِرِيْنَ ، وَصَلِّ وَسَلِّمْ وَبَارِكْ عَلَى سَيِّدِنَا مُحَمَّدٍ وَعَلَى أٰلِهٖ فِيْ كُلِّ وَقْتٍ وَ حِيْنٍ ، وَصَلِّ وَسَلِّمْ وَبَارِكْ عَلَى سَيِّدِنَا مُحَمَّدٍ وَ عَلَى أٰلِهٖ فِي الْمَلَإِ الْأَعْلَى إِلَى يَوْمِ الدِّيْنِ ، وَصَلِّ وَسَلِّمْ وَبَارِكْ عَلَى جَمِيْعِ الْأَنْبِيَآءِ وَ الْمُرْسَلِيْنَ ،

Allāhumma ṣalli wa sallim wa bārik `alā Sayyidinā Muḥammadin wa `alā Ālihī fī ‘l-awwalīn, Wa ṣalli wa sallim wa bārik `alā Sayyidinā Muḥammadin wa `alā Ālihī fī ‘l-ākhirīn, Wa ṣalli wa sallim wa bārik `alā Sayyidinā Muḥammadin wa `alā Ālihī fī kulli waqtin wa ḥīn, Wa ṣalli wa sallim wa bārik `alā Sayyidinā Muḥammadin wa `alā Ālihī fī ‘l-mala’i ‘l-`a‘lā ilā Yawmi ‘d-Dīn. Wa ṣalli wa sallim wa bārik `alā jamī`i ‘l-Anbiyā’i wa ‘l-Mursalīn,

O Allah send prayers upon, salute and bless our Master Muḥammad and his Family among the first, and send prayers upon, salute and bless our Master Muḥammad and his Family among the last, and send prayers upon, salute and bless our Master Muḥammad and his Family in every instant and interval, and send prayers upon, salute and bless our Master Muḥammad and his Family in the Highest Assembly until the Day of Judgement. , and send prayers upon, salute and bless the entirety of Prophets and Messengers,

وَعَلَى الْمَلٰٓئِكَةِ الْمُقَرَّبِيْنَ ، وَعَلَى عِبَادِ اللّٰهِ الصَّالِحِيْنَ مِنْ أَهْلِ السَّمَوَاتِ وَ أَهْلِ الْأَرَضِيْنَ ، وَرَضِيَ اللّٰهُ تَبَارَكَ وَ تَعَالَى عَنْ سَادَاتِنَا ذَوِي الْقَدْرِ الْجَلِيّ أَبِيْ بَكْرٍ وَعُمَرَ وَعُثْمَانَ وَعَلِيٍّ ، وَعَنْ سَائِرِ أَصْحَابِ رَسُوْلِ اللّٰهِ أَجْمَعِيْنَ مِنَ الْأَنْصَارِ وَالْمُهَاجِرِيْنَ ،

Wa `alā ‘l-Malā’ikati ‘l-Muqarrabīn, Wa `alā `ibādi ‘lLāhi ‘ṣ-Ṣālihīna min ahli ‘s-Samāwāti wa ahli ‘l-Araḍīn. Wa raḍiya ‘lLāhu tabāraka wa ta`ālā `an Sādātinā dha wī ‘l-qadri ‘l-jaliyyi Abī Bakrin wa `Umara wa `Uthmāna wa `Aliyyin, Wa `an sā’iri Aṣḥābi Rasūli ‘lLāhi ajma`īn, mina ‘l-Anṣāri wa ‘l-Muhājirīn,

Close Angels and Allah's Righteous Servants of the inhabitants of heavens and earths! The pleasure of Allah, Blessed and High, be upon our Worthy Masters Abu Bakr, Umar, Uthman, and Ali and the rest of the Companions of Allah's Messenger altogether, among the Helpers (Ansar), Migrants (Muhajireen)
