Text preservation and offline notes

All 140 pages are faithful high-resolution images of the source PDF. Arabic, diacritics, transliteration, translation, repetition and original layout have not been retyped. Page text is not selectable or reflowable. The unchanged original PDF is included.

Ornamental borders combine Andalusian-inspired geometry with Mughal and South Asian botanical pen flourishes. Ornaments add no sacred text.

Offline use requires one complete download. On iPhone, add this website to your Home Screen, open it from that icon while online, and tap “Save all pages offline”. Wait for “Ready offline” before disconnecting.
