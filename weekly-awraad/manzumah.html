108

Manżūmatu Asmā’i ’lLāhi ‘l-Ḥusnā li

SAYYIDĪ AD-DARDĪR

109

مَنْظُوْمَةُ أَسْمَاءِ اللّٰهِ الْحُسْنَى لِسَيِّدِي الدَّرْدِيْرُ

Manżūmatu Asmā’i ’lLāhi ‘l-Ḥusnā li Sayyidī ad-Dardīr

Invocation of al-Shaykh ad-Dardīr in which he calls on Allah by His beautiful 99 Names

Al-Shaykh al-Sawī commented on this Invocation of al-Shaykh ad-Dardīr by saying: it is really unique, because it contains complete prayers (calls on Allah) and splendid exalted secrets. (and) it is the last exalted knowledge that appeared from al-Shaykh ad-Dardīr’s tongue. It had been inspired to him in only one night. He just got up from his bed and wrote it. As been said, those righteous, the best to be harvested from their knowledges, is the very last of their speech, because that is the cream of their knowledges and secrets. Although all of their talks are of benefit. Moreover the composer himself said this invocation is of high virtues and any line of it (verse) is a complete litany by itself.

110
تَبَارَكْتَ يَا اللّٰهُ رَبِّيْ لَكَ الثَّنَافَحَمْدًا لِمَوْلَانَا وَشُكْرًا لِرَبِّنَا
Tabārakta Yā Allāhu Rabbī laka ‘th-ThanāFa ḥamdan li Mawlānā wa shukran li Rabbinā

You are Blessed O Allah, My Lord, Glory is Yours –And praise our Patron's and thanks is our Lord's.

بِأَسْمَائِكَ الْحُسْنَى وَأَسْرَارِهَا الَّتِيْأَقَمْتَ بِهَا الْأَكْوَانَ مِنْ حَضْرَةِ الْغِنَا
Bi Asmā’ika ‘l-Ḥusnā wa asrārihā ‘llatīAqamta bi hā ‘l-akwāna min ḥaḍrati ‘l-ghinā.

By Your Beautiful Names and their secrets by which –You established existence from Being Sufficiently-Rich.

فَنَدْعُوْكَ يَا اللّٰهُ يَا مُبْدِعَ الْوَرَىيَقِيْنًا يَقِيْنَا الْهَمَّ وَالْكَرْبَ وَالْعَنَا
Fa nad`ūka Yā Allāhu Yā Mubdi`a ‘l-warāYaqīnan yaqīna ‘l-hamma wa ‘l-karba wa ‘l-`anā.

So we call on You, O Allah, O Maker of Mortals –With certainty, protecting us from worry, anguish and trials.

وَيَا رَبُّ يَا رَحْمٰنُ هَبْنَا مَعَارِفًاوَلُطْفًا وَ إِحْسَانًا وَنُوْرًا يَعُمُّنَا
Wa Yā Rabbu Yā Raḥmānu habnā ma`ārifanWa luṭfan wa iḥsānan wa nūran ya`ummunā.

O Lord, O Merciful, bestow realisation on us – Subtleties, excellence and light encompassing us

وَسِرْ يَا رَحِيْمَ الْعَالَمِيْنَ بِجَمْعِنَاإِلَى حَضْرَةِ الْقُرْبِ الْمُقَدَّسِ وَاهْدِنَا
Wa sir Yā Raḥīma ‘l-`Ālamīna bi jam`ināIlā ḥaḍrati ‘l-qurbi ‘l-muqaddasi wa ‘hdinā

O Merciful of the Worlds, collectively march us – To the Presence of sanctified nearness, guide us

111
وَ يَا مَالِكُ مَلِّكْ جَمِيْعَ عَوَالِمِيلِرُوْحِيْ وَخَلِّصْ مِنْ سِوَاكَ عُقُوْلَنَا
Wa Yā Mālik mallik jamī`a `awālimīLi rūḥī wa khalliṣ min siwāka `uqūlanā

O King, take charge of all that I do – For my soul, purify my thoughts of other than You

وَقَدِّسْ أَيَا قُدُّوْسُ نَفْسِيْ مِنَ الْهَوَىوَسَلِّمْ جَمِيْعِيْ يَا سَلَامُ مِنَ الضَّنَا
Wa qaddis Ayā Quddūsu nafsī mina ‘l-hawāWa sallim jamī`ī Yā Salāmu mina ‘ḍ-ḍanā

And sanctify O Holy, my self from whim – And safeguard my entirety, O Safeguarder from exhaustion

وَ يَا مُؤْمِنُ هَبْ لِيْ أَمَانًا وَبَهْجَةًوَجَمِّلْ جَنَانِيْ يَا مُهَيْمِنُ بِالْمُنَى
Wa Yā Mu’minu hab lī amānan wa bahjatanWa jammil janānī Yā Muhayminu bi ‘l-munā

O Faithful bestow upon me safety and delight – And beautify my insides O Preserver, with longing

وَجُدْ لِيْ بِعِزٍّ يَا عَزِيْزُ وَقُوَّةٍوَبِالْجَبْرِ يَا جَبَّارُ بَدِّدْ عَدُوَّنَا
Wa jud lī bi `izzin Yā `Azīzu wa quwwatinWa bi ‘l-jabri Yā Jabbāru baddid `aduwwanā

Lavish me with honor, O Mighty, and power – With might O Compeller, disperse our enemies

وَكَبِّرْ شُءُوْنِيْ فِيْكَ يَا مُتَكَبِّرُوَيَا خَالِقَ الْأَكْوَانِ بِالْفَيْضِ عُمَّنَا
Wa kabbir shu’ūnī fīka Yā MutakabbiruWa Yā Khāliqa ‘l-akwāni bi ‘l-fayḍi `ummanā

Enhance my affairs with You O Supreme – O Creator of Existences, inundate us with abundance

112
وَ يَا بَارِئُ احْفَظْنَا مِنَ الْخَلْقِ كُلِّهِمْبِفَضْلِكَ وَاكْشِفْ يَا مُصَوِّرُ كَرْبَنَا
Wa Yā Bāri’u ‘ḥfaẓnā min ‘l-khalqi kullihimBi faḍlika wa ‘kshif Yā Muṣawwiru karbanā

By Your grace, and remove our worries – O Fashioner, protect us from creation, all of it

وَبِالْغَفْرِ يَا غَفَّارُ مَحِّصْ ذُنُوْبَنَاوَبِالْقَهْرِ يَا قَهَّارُ اقْهَرْ عَدُوَّنَا
Wa bi ‘l-ghafri Yā Ghaffāru maḥḥiṣ dhunūbanāWa bi ‘l-qahri Yā Qahhāru ‘qhar `aduwwanā

With forgiveness, O Forgiver, eradicate our sins – With force O Subduer, vanquish our enemies

وَ هَبْ لِيْ أَيَا وَهَّابُ عِلْمًا وَحِكْمَةًوَلِلرِّزْقِ يَا رَزَّاقُ وَسِّعْ وَجُدْ لَنَا
Wa hab lī Ayā Wahhābu `ilman wa ḥikmatanWa li ‘r-rizqi Yā Razzāqu wassi` wa jud lanā

Provision, O Provider, expand and improve for us – Bestow me, O Bestower, with knowledge and wisdom

وَبِالْفَتْحِ يَا فَتَّاحُ عَجِّلْ تكَرُّمًاوَ بِالْعِلْمِ نَوِّرْ يَا عَلِيْمُ قُلُوْبَنَا
Wa bi ‘l-fatḥi Yā Fattāḥu `ajjil takarrumaWa bi ‘l-`ilmi nawwir Yā `Alīmu qulūbanā

And with knowledge illuminate, O All-Knowing, our hearts – With opening, O Opener, expedite with deference

وَيَا قَابِضُ اقْبِضْنَا عَلَى خَيْرِ حَالَةٍوَيَا بَاسِطَ الْأَرْزَاقِ بَسْطًا لِرِزْقِنَا
Wa Yā Qābiḍu ‘qbiḍnā `alā khayri ḥālatinWa Yā Bāsiṭu ‘l-arzāqi basṭan li rizqinā

O Extender of Provisions, extend our provision – O Grasper, seize us in the best of states

113
وَيَا خَافِضُ اخْفِضْ لِي الْقُلُوْبَ تَحَبُّبًاوَيَا رَافِعُ ارْفَعْ ذِكْرَنَا وَاعْلِ قَدْرَنَا
Wa Yā Khāfiḍu ‘khfiḍ li ‘l-qulūba taḥabbubaWa Yā Rāfi`u ‘rfa` dhikranā wa` ‘li qadranā

O Abaser, humble hearts to me with caress – O Exalter, exalt our mention and raise our status

وَ بِالزُّهْدِ وَالتَّقْوَى مُعِزٌّ أَعِزَّنَاوَذَلِّلْ بِصَفْوٍ يَا مُذِلُّ نُفُوْسَنَا
Wa bi ‘z-zuhdi wa ‘t-taqwā mu`izzun a`izzanāWa dhallil bi ṣafwin Yā Mudhillu nufūsanā

With devotion and piety, Honorer, honor us – Humble our egos in serenity, O Degrader

وَنَفِّذْ بِحَقٍّ يَا سَمِيْعُ مَقَالَتِيْوَبَصِّرْ فُؤَادِيْ يَا بَصِيْرُ بِعَيْبِنَا
Wa naffidh bi ḥaqqin Yā Samī`u maqālatīWa baṣṣir fu’ādī Yā Baṣīru bi `aybinā

Execute with appropriateness, O All Hearing, my statements – Enlighten my heart, O All Seeing, to my faults

وَيَا حَكَمُ يَا عَدْلُ حَكِّمْ قُلُوْبَنَابِعَدْلِكَ فِي الْأَشْيَاءِ وَبِالرُّشْدِ قَوِّنَا
Wa Yā Ḥakamun Yā `Adlu ḥakkim qulūbanāBi `adlika fi ‘l-ashyā’i wa bi ‘r-rushdi qawwinā

O Judge, O Just, rule over our hearts – With justice in all things and empower us with rationality

وَحُفَّ بِلُطْفٍ يَا لَطِيْفُ أَحِبَّتِيْوَ تَوِّجْهُمُوْ بِالنُّوْرِ كَيْ يُدْرِكُوا الْمُنَى
Wa ḥuffa bi luṭfin Yā Laṭīfu aḥibbatīWa tawwij humū bi ‘n-nūri kay yudriku ‘l-munā

Surround us with gentleness, O Gentle, my Love – Crown them with light to reach what they long for

114
وَكُنْ يَا خَبِيْرُ كَاشِفًا لِكُرُوْبِنَاوَ بِالْحِلْمِ خَلِّقْ يَا حَلِيْمُ نُفُوْسَنَا
Wa kun Yā Khabīru kāshifan li kurūbināwa bi ‘l-ḥilmi khalliq Yā Ḥalīmu nufūsanā

Be, O Aware, a removal of our distress – With forbearance, O Forbearing perfume our souls

وَ بِالْعِلْمِ عَظِّمْ يَا عَظِيْمُ شُءُوْنَنَاوَ فِيْ مَقْعَدِ الصِّدْقِ الْأَجَلِّ أَحِلَّنَا
Wa bi ‘l-`ilmi `aẓẓim Yā `Aẓīmu shu’ūnanāWa fī maq`adi ‘ṣ-ṣidqi ‘l-ajalli aḥillanā

With knowledge aggrandize,O Great,our affairs – In the seat of most sublime truthfulness,make our state

غَفُوْرٌ شَكُوْرٌ لَمْ تَزَلْ مُتَفَضِّلًافَبِالشُّكْرِ وَالْغُفْرَانِ مَوْلَايَ خُصَّنَا
Ghafūrun shakūrun lam tazal mutafaḍḍilāFa bi ‘sh-shukri wa ‘l-ghufrāni mawlāya khuṣṣanā

Forgiving and Appreciative, favoritism does not cease – So with gratitude and forgiveness, my Master select us

عَلِيٌّ كَبِيْرٌ جَلَّ عَنْ وَهْمِ وَاهِمٍفَسُبْحَانَكَ اللَّهُمَّ عَنْ وَصْفِ مَنْ جَنَى
`Aliyyun kabīrun jalla `an wahmi wāhiminFa subhānaka ‘lLāhumma `an waṣfi man janā

High and Magnificent, beyond all imagination – So Glorified are You, O Allah, from the descriptions of the injurious

وَكُنْ لِيْ حَفِيْظًا يَا حَفِيْظُ مِنَ الْبَلَامُقِيْتٌ أَقِتْنَا خَيْرَ قُوْتٍ وَهَنِّنَا
Wa kun lī ḥafīẓan Yā Ḥafīẓu mina ‘l-balāMuqītun aqitnā khayra qūtin wa hanninā

Be for me a protection, O Protector, from misfortune – Maintainer, nourish us with the best and make us happy

115
وَ أَنْتَ غِيَاثِيْ يَا حَسِيْبُ مِنَ الرَّدَىوَ أَنْتَ مَلَاذِيْ يَا جَلِيْلُ وَحَسْبُنَا
Wa anta ghiyāthī Yā Ḥasību mina ‘r-radāWa Anta malādhī Yā Jalīlu wa ḥasbunā

You are my succour, O Reckoner, from destruction – You are my refuge, O Sublime, and our sufficiency

وَ جُدْ يَا كَرِيْمُ بِالْعَطَا مِنْكَ وَالرِّضَاوَتَزْكِيَةِ الْأَخْلَاقِ وَالْجُوْدِ وَالْغِنَى
Wa jud Yā Karīmu bi ‘l`aṭā min ka wa ‘r-riḍāWa tazkiyati ‘l-akhlāqi wa ‘l-jūdi wa ‘l-ghinā

Confer, O Generous, by a gift from You and contentment – Purified morals, generosity and prosperity

رَقِيْبٌ عَلَيْنَا فَاعْفُ عَنَّا وَعَافِنَاوَيَسِّرْ عَلَيْنَا يَا مُجِيْبُ أُمُوْرَنَا
Raqībun `alaynā fa`fu `annā wa `āfināWa yassir `alaynā Yā Mujību umūranā

Watcher over us, pardon us and cure us – Ease for us, O Responder, all of our affairs

وَ يَا وَاسِعًا وَسِّعْ لَنَا الْعِلْمَ وَالْعَطَاحَكِيْمًا أَنِلْنَا حِكْمَةً مِنْكَ تَهْدِنَا
Wa Yā Wāsi`an wassi` lana ‘l-`ilma wa ‘l-`aṭāḤakīman anilnā hikmatan min ka tahdinā

O Vast, expand for us knowledge and presents – Wise, grant us wisdom from You and guide us

وَدُوْدٌ فَجُدْ بِالْوُدِّ مِنْكَ تكَرُّمًاعَلَيْنَا وَشَرِّفْ يَا مَجِيْدُ شُءُوْنَنَا
Wadūdun fajud bi ‘l-wuddi min ka takarrumā`Alaynā wa sharrif Yā Majīdu shu’ūnanā

Loving, enhance the love from You generously – To us, and dignify, O Majestic, all our matters

116
وَ يَا بَاعِثُ ابْعَثْنَا عَلَى خَيْرِ حَالَةٍشَهِيْدٌ فَأَشْهِدْنَا عُلَاكَ بِجَمْعِنَا
Wa Yā Bā`ithu ‘b`athnā `alā khayri ḥālatinShahīdun fa’ash’hidnā `ulāka bi jam`inā

O Resurrector, resurrect us in the best of states – Witness, have us witness Your Highness in our assembly

وَيَا حَقُّ حَقِّقْنَا بِسِرٍّ مُقَدَّسٍوَكِيْلٌ تَوَكَّلْنَا عَلَيْكَ بِكَ اكْفِنَا
Wa Yā Ḥaqqu ḥaqqiqnā bi sirrin muqaddasinWakīlun tawakkalnā `alayka bi ka ‘kfinā

O Truth, actualize us by a sanctified secret – Benefactor, we rely on You, by You we are sufficed

قَوِيٌّ مَتِيْنٌ قَوِّ عَزْمِيْ وَهِمَّتِيْوَلِيٌّ حَمِيْدٌ لَيْسَ إِلَّا لَكَ الثَّنَا
Qawiyyun Matīnun qawwi `azmī wa himmatīWaliyyun Ḥamīdun laysa illā laka ‘th-Thanā

Almighty Relentless, strengthen my resolve and aspiration – Safeguard, Praiseworthy, no one but You deserve praise

وَيَا مُحْصِيَ الْأَشْيَاءَ يَا مُبْدِئَ الْوَرَىتَعَطَّفْ عَلَيْنَا بِالْمَسَرَّةِ وَالْهَنَا
Wa Yā Muḥṣiya ‘l-ashyā’a Yā Mubdi’a ‘l-warāTa`aṭṭaf `alaynā bi ‘l-masarrati wa ‘l-hanā

O Numerator of things, O Originator of mortals – Favor us with happiness and mirth

أَعِدْنَا بِنُوْرٍ يَا مُعِيْدُ وَأَحْيِنَاعَلَى الدِّيْنِ يَا مُحْيِي الْأَنَامِ مِنَ الْفَنَا
A`idnā bi nūrin Yā Mu`īdu wa aḥyinā`Alā ‘d-Dīni Yā Muḥyi ‘l-anāmi mina ‘l-fanā

Return us with light, O Restorer, and revive us - On the religion, O Life-Giver of creatures, from annihilation

117
مُمِيْتٌ أَمِتْنِيْ مُسْلِمًا وَمُوَحِّدًاوَشَرِّفْ بِذَا قَدْرِيْ كَمَا أَنْتَ رَبُّنَا
Mumītun amitnī musliman wa muwaḥḥidāWa sharrif bidhā qadrī kamā anta Rabbunā

Life-Taker, take me as a Muslim, affirming Unity – Honor thus my destiny as You are our Lord

وَيَا حَيُّ يَا قَيُّوْمُ قَوِّمْ أُمُوْرَنَاوَيَا وَاجِدُ أَنْتَ الْغَنِيُّ فَأَغْنِنَا
Wa Yā Ḥayyu Yā Qayyūmu qawwim umūranāWa Yā Wājidu anta ‘l-Ghaniyyu fa aghninā

O Living, O Self-Subsisting, rectify our affairs – O Concerned, You are Self-Sufficient, so enrich us

وَيَا مَاجِدُ شَرِّفْ بِمَجْدِكَ قَدْرَنَاوَيَا وَاحِدُ فَرِّجْ كُرُوْبِيْ وَغَمَّنَا
Wa Yā Mājidu sharrif bi majdika qadranāWa Yā Wāḥidu farrij kurūbī wa ghammanā

O Exalted, with Your glory honor our ability – O Unique, dispel our worry and grief

وَيَا صَمَدُ فَوَّضْتُ أَمْرِيْ إِلَيْكَ لَاتَكِلْنِيْ لِنَفْسِيْ وَاهْدِنَا رَبِّ سُبْلَنَا
Wa Yā Ṣamadu fawwaḍtu amrī ilayka lāTakilnī li nafsī wa ‘hdinā Rabbi sublanā

O Absolute, I entrust my affair to You – Do not leave me to my self and guide us, Lord, along our path

وَيَا قَادِرُ اقْدِرْنَا عَلَى صَدْمَةِ الْعِدَاوَمُقْتَدِرٌ خَلِّصْ مِنَ الْغَيْرِ سِرَّنَا
Wa Yā Qādiru ‘qdirnā `alā ṣadmati ‘l-`idāWa Muqtadirun khalliṣ mina ‘l-ghayri sirranā

O Able, enable us to repel enemy attacks - O Omnipotent, sanctify our secrets from others

118
وَقَدِّمْ أُمُوْرِيْ يَا مُقَدِّمُ هَيْبَةًوَأَخِّرْ عِدَانَا يَا مُؤَخِّرُ بِالْعَنَا
Wa qaddim umūrī Yā Muqaddimu haybatanWa akhkhir `idānā Yā Mu’akhkhiru bi ‘l-`anā

Expedite my affair, O Expeditor, prestigiously – Delay our enemies O Delayer, with obstacles

وَيَا أَوَّلُ مِنْ غَيْرِ بَدْءٍ وَأٰخِرٌبِغَيْرِ انْتِهَاءٍ أَنْتَ فِيْ الْكُلِّ حَسْبُنَا
Wa Yā Awwalu min ghayri bad’in wa ākhirunBi ghayri ‘ntihā’in anta fī ‘l-kulli ḥasbunā

O Eternal, without beginning, Everlasting – Without end, You suffice us in

وَيَا ظَاهِرًا فِيْ كُلِّ شَيْءٍ شُءُوْنُهُوَيَا بَاطِنًا بِالْغَيْبِ لَا زِلْتَ مُحْسِنَا
Wa Yā Ẓāhiran fī kulli shay’in shu’ūnuhūWa Yā Bātinan bi ‘l-ghaybi lā zilta muḥsinā

O Manifest in the affairs of everything – O Hidden unseen, You never cease being good to us

وَيَا وَالِيًا لَسْنَا لِغَيْرِكَ نَنْتَمِيْفَبِالنَّصْرِ يَا مُتَعَالِيًا كُنْ مُعِزَّنَا
Wa yā wāliyan lasnā li ghayrika nantamīFa bi ‘n-naṣri Yā Muta`āliyan kun mu`izzanā

O Governor, we associate ourselves with no other – So with help, O Most High, be our fortifier

وَيَا بَرُّ يَا تَوَّابُ جُدْ لِيْ بِتَوْبَةٍنَصُوْحٍ بِهَا تَمْحُوْ عَظَائِمَ جُرْمِنَا
Wa Yā Barru Yā Tawwābu jud lī bi tawbatinNaṣūhin bi hā tamḥū `aẓā’ima jurminā

O Beneficent, O Accepter of Repentance, bless me with Repentance Sincere, eradicate thereby our enormities

119
وَمُنْتَقِمٌ هَاكَ انْتَقِمْ مِنْ عَدُوِّنَاعَفُوٌّ رَؤُوْفٌ عَافِنَا وَارْأَفْ بِنَا
Wa Muntaqimun hāka ‘ntaqim min `aduwwinā`Afuwwun Ra’ūfun `āfinā wa ‘r ’afan binā

O Avenger, take revenge on our enemies – Pardoner Compassionate, cure us and be kind to us

وَيَا مَالِكَ الْمُلْكِ الْعَظِيْمِ بِقَهْرِهِوَيَا ذَا الْجَلَالِ اُلْطُفْ بِنَا فِيْ أُمُوْرِنَا
Wa Yā Mālik ‘l-Mulki ‘l-`Aẓīmi bi qahrihīWa Yā Dhā ‘l-Jalāli ulṭuf bi nā fī umūrinā

O King of Awesome Dominion, with its subdual – O Lord of Majesty, be gentle with us in our affairs

وَيَا مُقْسِطُ بِالْاِسْتِقَامَةِ قَوِّنَاوَيَا جَامِعُ فَاجْمَعْ عَلَيْكَ قُلُوْبَنَا
Wa Yā Muqsiṭu bi ‘l-istiqāmati qawwināWa Yā Jāmi‘u fajma` `alayka qulūbanā

O Equitable, with uprightness strengthen us – O Assembler, assemble our hearts for You

غَنِيٌّ وَمُغْنٍ ، أَغْنِنَا بِكَ سَيِّدِيْوَيَا مَانِعُ امْنَعْ كُلَّ كَرْبٍ يُهِمُّنَا
Ghaniyyun wa Mughnin aghninā bi ka SayyidīWa Yā Māni`u ‘mna` kulla karbin yuhimmunā

Self-Sufficient, Enricher, enrich us with You, my Master – O Preventer, ward off every distress and worry

وَيَا ضَارُّ ضُرَّ الْمُعْتَدِيْنَ بِظُلْمِهِمْوَيَا نَافِعُ انْفَعْنَا بِأَنْوَارِ دِيْنِنَا
Wa Yā Ḍārru ḍurra ‘l-mu`tadīna bi ẓulmihimWa Yā Nāfi`u ‘nfa`nā bi anwāri Dīninā

O Afflicter, afflict aggressors with their own wrongdoing – O Beneficial, benefit us with the lights of our religion

120
وَيَا نُوْرُ نَوِّرْ ظَاهِرِيْ وَسَرَائِرِيْبِحُبِّكَ يَا هَادِيْ وَقَوِّمْ طَرِيْقَنَا
Wa Yā Nūru nawwir ẓāhirī wa sarā’irīBi ḥubbi ka Yā Hādī wa qawwim ṭarīqanā

O Light, illuminate my disclosures and my secrets – With Your love, O Guide, establish our way

بَدِيْعٌ فَأَتْحِفْنَا بَدَائِعَ حِكْمَةٍوَ يَا بَاقِيًا بِكَ أَبْقِنَا فِيْكَ أَفْنِنَا
Badī`un fa at ḥifnā badā’i`a ḥikmatinWa Yā Bāqiyan bi ka abqinā fīka afninā.

O Original, open for us the origins of wisdom – O Forever Remaining, by You let us remain forever annihilated in You.

وَ يَا وَارِثًا وَرِّثْنِ عِلْمًا وَحِكْمَةًرَشِيْدٌ فَأَرْشِدْنَا إِلَى طُرُقِ الثَّنَا
Wa Yā Wārithan warrithni `ilman wa ḥikmatanRashīdun fa arshidnā ilā turuqi ‘th-thanā.

O Inheritor, bequeath me with knowledge and wisdom – Right-Guiding, guide us to the paths of praise.

وَأَفْرِغْ عَلَيْنَا الصَّبْرَ بِالشُّكْرِ وَالرِّضَاوَحُسْنَ يَقِيْنٍ يَا صَبُوْرُ وَوَفِّنَا
Wa afrigh `alayna ‘ṣ-ṣabra bi ‘sh-shukri wa ’r-riḍāWa ḥusna yāqīnin Yā Ṣabūru wa waffinā.

Pour patience upon us, with gratitude and contentment – And the best of certainty, O Patient, in pay us in full.

بِأَسْمَائِكَ الْحُسْنَى دَعَوْنَاكَ سَيِّدِيْتَقَبَّلْ دُعَانَا رَبَّنَا وَاسْتَجِبْلَنَا
Bi Asmā’ika ‘l-Ḥusnā da`awnāka SayyidīTaqabbal du`ānā Rabbanā wa ‘stajib lanā.

With Your beautiful names, we called You, my Master – Accept our call, our Lord, and respond to us.

121
بِأَسْرَارِهَا عَمِّرْ فُؤَادِيْ وَظَاهِرِيْوَحَقِّقْ بِهَا رُوْحِيْ لِأَظْفَرَ بِالْمُنَى
Bi asrārihā `ammir fu’ādī wa ẓāhirīWa ḥaqqiq bi hā rūhī li’aẓfara bi ‘l-munā

By their secrets condition my inward and out – Actualize by them my soul so I obtain my desire

وَنَوِّرْ بِهَا سَمْعِيْ وَشَمِّيْ وَنَاظِرِيْوَ قَوِّ بِهَا ذَوْقِيْ وَلَمْسِيْ وَعَقْلَنَا
Wa nawwir bi hā sam`ī wa shammī wa nāẓirīWa qawwi bi hā dhawqī wa lamsī wa `aqlanā.

Illuminate by them my hearing, smelling and seeing – Strengthen by them my taste, touch and our consciousness.

وَيَسِّرْ بِهَا أَمْرِيْ وَقَوِّ عَزَائِمِيْوَزَكِّ بِهَا نَفْسِيْ وَفَرِّجْ كُرُوْبَنَا
Wa yassir bi hā amrī wa qawwi `azā’imīWa zakki bi hā nafsī wa farrij kurūbanā

Ease by them my affair and strengthen my resolve – Purify by them my self and dispel our concerns.

وَ وَسِّعْ بِهَا عِلْمِيْ وَرِزْقِيْ وَهِمَّتِيْوَحَسِّنْ بِهَا خَلْقِيْ وَخُلُقِيْ مَعَ الْهَنَا
Wa wassi` bi hā `ilmī wa rizqī wa himmatīWa ḥassin bi hā khalqī wa khuluqī ma`a ‘l-hanā

Expand by them my knowledge, sustainance and aspiration – Beautify by them my form and character with pleasure.

وَ هَبْ لِيْ بِهَا حُبًّا جَلِيْلًا مُجَمَّلًاوَزِدْنِيْ بِفَرْطِ الْحُبِّ فِيْكَ تَفَنُّن
Wa hab lī bi hā ḥubban jalīlan mujammalāWa zid nī bi farṭi ‘l-ḥubbi fīka tafannunā

Bestow upon me by them beautified supreme love –Increase me in excessive love for You, annihilating us.

122
وَ هَبْ لِيْ أَيَا رَبَّاهُ كَشْفًا مُقَدَّسًالِأَدْرِيْ بِهِ سِرَّ الْبَقَاءِ مَعَ الْفَنَا
Wa hab lī Ayā Rabbāhu kashfan muqaddasāLi adrī bi hi sirra ‘l-baqā’i ma`a ‘l-fanā

Bestow upon me, O Lord of a sanctified unveiling – So I reach by it the secret of remaining in annihilation.

وَجُدْ لِيْ بِجَمْعِ الْجَمْعِ فَضْلًا وَمِنَّةًوَدَاوِ بِوَصْلِ الْوَصْلِ رُوْحِيْ مِنَ الضَّنَا
Wa jud lī bi jami` ‘l-jam`i faḍlan wa minnatanWa dāwi bi waṣli ‘l-waṣli rūḥī mina ‘ḍ-ḍanā

Bless me in joining the assembly with virtue and excellence – Cure by the arrival my soul of grief

وَ سِرْبِيْ عَلَى النَّهْجِ الْقَوِيْمِ مُوَحِّدًاوَ فِيْ حَضْرَةِ الْقُدْسِ الْمَنِيْعِ أَحِلَّنَا
Wa sirbī `alā ‘n-nahji ‘l-qawīmi muwaḥḥidāWa fī ḥaḍrati ‘l-qudsi ‘l-manī`i aḥillanā

Advance me through the proper trail, a Unitarian – In the Safe Holy Presence permit us

وَ مُنَّ عَلَيْنَا يَا وَدُوْدُ بِجَذْبَةٍبِهَا نَلْحَقُ الْأَقْوَامَ مَنْ سَارَ قَبْلَنَا ثلاثا
Wa munna `alaynā Yā Wadūdu bi jadhbatinBihā nalḥaqu ‘l-aqwāma man sāra qablanā (x3)

Grant us, O Loving, a tug – By which we reach those traversing before us.

وَ صَلِّ وَسَلِّمْ سَيِّدِيْ كُلَّ لَمْحَةٍ .عَلَى الْمُصْطَفَى خَيْرِ الْبَرَايَا نَبِيِّنَا
Wa ṣalli wa sallim Sayyidī kulla lamḥatin`Alā ‘l-Muṣṭafā khayri ‘l-Barāyā Nabiyyinā.

And Prayers and Salutations, my Master, in every glimpse – Upon the Chosen One, the Best of Creatures, our Prophet.

123
وَ صَلِّ عَلَى الْأَمْلَاكِ وَالرُّسْلِ كُلِّهِمْوَآلِهِمُوْ وَالصَّحْبِ جَمْعًا وَعُمَّنَا
Wa ṣalli `alā ‘l-Amlāki wa ‘r-Rusli kullihimWa Ālihimū wa ṣ-Ṣaḥbi jam`an wa `ummanā

And Prayers upon the Angels and Messengers, all of them – Their Family and Companions altogether, and include us.

وَ سَلِّمْ عَلَيْهِمْ كُلَّمَا قَالَ قَائِلٌتَبَارَكْتَ يَا اَللّٰهُ رَبِّيْ لَكَ الثَّنَا
Wa sallim `alayhim kullamā qāla qā’ilunTabārakta Yā Allāhu Rabbī laka ‘th-Thanā

And Salute them everytime a speaker speaks –Blessed are You, O Allah, my Lord, to You is all praise!

اَللَّهُمَّ إِنَّكَ قُلْتَ وَقَوْلُكَ الْحَقُّ فِيْ مُحْكَمِ كِتَابِكَ الْمُنْزَلِ، عَلَى لِسَانِ نَبِيِّكَ الْمُرْسَلِ،، وَلِلّٰهِ الْأَسْمَآءُ الْحُسْنَى فَادْعُوْهُ بِهَاۖ ،، فَدَعَوْنَاكَ لِجَلْبِ الْخَيْرِ وَدَفْعِ الشَّرِّ كَمَا أَمَرْتَنَا فَاسْتَجِبْ لَنَا يَا مَوْلَانَا كَمَا وَعَدْتَّنَا إِنَّكَ لَا تُخْلِفُ الْمِيْعَادَ.

Allāhumma innaka qulta wa qawluka ‘l-Ḥaqqu, fī muḥkami Kitābi ka ‘l-Munzali, `alā lisāni Nabiyyika ‘l-Mursali, wa li ‘lLāhi ‘l-Asmā’u ‘l-Ḥusnā fa ‘d`ūhu bi hā. Fa da`awnāka li jalbi ‘l-khayri wa daf`i ‘sh-sharri kamā amartanā, fa ‘stajib lanā Yā Mawlānā kamā wa `adtanā, innaka lā tukhlifu ‘l-mī`ād.

O Allah, You surely said, and Your speech is Truth, in the decisive verses of Your revealed book, upon the tongue of Your Messenger Prophet, "To Allah belong the most beautiful names, so call upon Him with them," so we called upon You to procure good and repel evil, as You have ordered us, so respond to us, O our Master as You promised us, surely You never renege on promises!

Sura Al’Araf Verse 180

124

فَاعْلَمْ أَنَّهٗ لَآ إِلٰهَ إِلَّا اللّٰهُ مِائة مرّة

Fa ‘`lam anna hū lā ilāha ilLa ‘lLāhu (x 100)

So know, there is no god but Allah!

Sura Muhammed Verse 19

125

HERE THE SEEKER PRACTISES THE SILENT CALL (PRAYER) BY PUTTING BOTH HANDS ON THE CHEST, AND SILENTLY READS THIS PRAYER

اَللَّهُمَّ صَلِّ وَسَلِّمْ وَبَارِكْ عَلَى سَيِّدِنَا مُحَمَّدٍ وَعَلَى ألِهٖ وَ صَحْبِهِ وَسَلِّمْ، اَلصَّلَاةُ وَ السَّلَامُ عَلَيْكَ يَا رَسُوْلَ اللّٰهِ، اَلصَّلَاةُ وَالسَّلَامُ عَلَيْكَ يَا حَبِيْبَ اللّٰهِ، اَلصَّلَاةُ وَ السَّلَامُ عَلَيْكَ يَا نَبِيَّ اللّٰهِ، اَلْعَظَمَةُ لِلّٰهِ تَكْبِيْرًا، اَللّٰهُ أَكْبَرُ، اَللّٰهُ أَكْبَرُ، لَآ إِلٰهَ إِلَّا اللّٰهُ وَ اللّٰهُ أَكْبَرُ، وَ لِلّٰهِ الْحَمْدُ.

Allāhumma ṣalli wa sallim wa bārik `alā Sayyidinā Muḥammadin wa `alā Ālihī wa Ṣaḥbihī wa sallim. Aṣ-Ṣalātu wa ‘s-salāmu `alayka Yā Rasūla ‘lLāhi. Aṣ-Ṣalātu wa ‘s-Salāmu `alayka Yā Ḥabība ‘lLāhi. Aṣ-Ṣalātu wa ‘s-Salāmu `alayka Yā Nabiyya ‘lLāhi. Al-`Aẓamatu li ‘lLāhi takbīrā. Allāhu Akbar, Allāhu Akbar. Lā ilāha illa ‘lLāhu wa ‘lLāhu Akbar wa li ‘lLāhi ‘l-Ḥamd.

O Allah, send prayers, salute and bless our Master Muḥammad and his Family and Companions! Prayers and Peace be upon you, O Messenger of Allah! Prayers and Peace be upon you, O Beloved of Allah! Prayers and Peace be upon you, O Prophet of Allah! Supreme Greatness is Allah's alone and ever Greater! Allah is the Greatest! Allah is Greatest! There is no god but Allah, and Allah is the Greatest and for Allah is all praise!
