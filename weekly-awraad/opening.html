بِسْمِ اللّٰهِ الرَّحْمٰنِ الرَّحِيْمِ

The opening Silsilah Fātiḥa on the Masters of the Alawi Ḥusayni Ninowy Tariqah.

إِلَى حَضْرَةِ النَّبِيِّ الْمُصْطَفَى صَلَّى اللّٰهُ تَعَالَى عَلَيْهِ وَعَلَى أٰلِهٖ وَسَلَّمَ وَ إِلَى أٰلِ بَيْتِهِ الطَّاهِرِيْنَ وَذُرِّيَتِهِ الصّٰلِحِيْنَ وَ إِلَى آبَائِنَا وَ مَشَايِخِنَا أَجْمَعِيْنَ الفَاتِحَةُ

lā Ḥaḍrati ‘n-Nabiyyil Mustafā ṣallallaho ta`ālā `alayhi wa `alā Ālihī wa sallama wa ilā Āli baytihi ‘t-tāhirina wa dhurriyatihi ‘ṣ-Ṣalihina wa ilā Ābā’ina wa mashāyikhinā ajma`īn. Al Fātiḥa

To the presence of the Chosen Prophet, Allah the Exalted send prayers and peace upon him, to his purified household and pious progeny and to our fathers and elders, altogether… the Fātihah…

بِسْمِ اللّٰهِ الرَّحْمٰنِ الرَّحِيْمِ ـ اَلْحَمْدُ لِلّٰهِ رَبِّ الْعٰلَمِيْنَۙ ۝۱ الرَّحْمٰنِ الرَّحِيْمِ ۝۲ مٰلِكِ يَوْمِ الدِّيْنِؕ ۝۳ إِيَّاكَ نَعْبُدُ وَإِيَّاكَ نَسْتَعِيْنُؕ ۝۴ اِهْدِنَا الصِّرَاطَ الْمُسْتَقِيْمَۙ ۝۵ صِرَاطَ الَّذِيْنَ أَنْعَمْتَ عَلَيْهِمْۙ ۵ غَيْرِ الْمَغْضُوْبِ عَلَيْهِمْ وَلَا الضَّآلِّيْنَ ۝۷

Praise Allah, Lord of the Worlds.(1) The Profoundly Merciful, The Continually Merciful.(2) Owner of the Day of Doctrine {Judgement}(3) You Alone we worship and You Alone we beseech(4) Show us the straight path(5) The path of those you favour (6), not those with wrath upon them nor those astray

6. Āyat al-Kursī [7 times]

بِسْمِ اللّٰهِ الرَّحْمٰنِ الرَّحِيْمِ

اَللّٰهُ لَآ إِلٰهَ إِلَّا هُوَۚ اَلْحَيُّ الْقَيُّوْمُۚ ۵ لَا تَأْخُذُهٗ سِنَةٌ وَّلَا نَوْمٌؕ لَهٗ مَا فِي السَّمٰوٰتِ وَمَا فِي الْأَرْضِؕ مَنْ ذَا الَّذِيْ يَشْفَعُ عِنْدَهٗٓ إِلَّا بِإِذْنِهٖؕ يَعْلَمُ مَا بَيْنَ أَيْدِيْهِمْ وَمَا خَلْفَهُمْۚ وَلَا يُحِيْطُوْنَ بِشَيْءٍ مِّنْ عِلْمِهٖٓ إِلَّا بِمَا شَآءَۚ وَسِعَ كُرْسِيُّهُ السَّمٰوٰتِ وَالْأَرْضَۚ وَلَا يَئُوْدُهٗ حِفْظُهُمَاۚ وَهُوَ الْعَلِيُّ الْعَظِيْمُ ۝۲۵۵ ـ سَبْعًا

Bismi ‘lLāhi ‘r-Raḥmāni ‘r-Raḥīm.

Allāhu Lā ilāha illā Huwa ‘l-Ḥayyu ‘l-Qayyum. Lā ta’khudhu hu sinatuw wa lā nawm. Lahū mā fi ‘s-Samāwāti wamā fi ‘l-Arḍ. Man dhā ‘ladhī yashfa`u `indahū illā bi idhnihī. Ya`lamu mā bayna aydīhim wa mā khalfa hum wa lā yuḥīṭūna bi shay’i ‘m-min `Ilmihī illā bi mā shā’. Wasi`a Kursiyyuhu ‘s-Samāwāti wa ‘l-Arḍ, wa lā ya’ūduhū ḥifẓuhumā, wa Huwa ‘l-`Aliyyu ‘l-`Aẓīm.

In the name of Allah, The Profoundly Merciful, The Continually Merciful

Allah, there is no god but He, the Living, Self-Subsisting. Neither sleep nor slumber overtakes Him. He possesses all that is in the heavens and earth. Who intercedes with Him without His permission? He knows what is before them and after them and they grasp nothing of His knowledge but what He wills. His throne spans the heavens and earth and it tires Him not maintaining them. And He is the Great, the High! .

28

29

اَللَّهُمَّ أَفْنِنَا فِيْ وِصَالِهِ وَعِشْقِهِ وَأَسْقِنَا مِنْ كَاسَاتِ مَحَبَّتِهِ وَ ارْزُقْنَا يَا مَوْلَانَا فِي الدَّارَيْنِ رُؤْيَتَهُ وَ أَحْيِنَا عَلَى اتِّبَاعِ سُنَّتِهِ وَأَمِتْنَا عَلَى مِلَّتِهِ وَاجْعَلْنَا مِنْ رُفَقَائِهِ ، وَشَفِّعْهُ فِيْنَا كَمَا يُحِبُّ أَنْ يُشَفَّعَ فِيْنَا وَاجْعَلْنَا مِنْ خِيَارِ الْمُصَلِّيْنَ وَ الْمُسَلِّمِيْنَ عَلَيْهِ وَالْحَمْدُ لِلّٰهِ رَبِّ الْعَالَمِيْنَ.

31

then the realms' gardens became pleasing by the blooming of his beauty and the domain of power effused by the flood of his lights such that there was nothing save that he was in charge of it; if not for the keystone, all others would fall, as it is said, a prayer linked to You, from You to him just as he deserves!

36

Wa ṣalli wa sallim wa bārik `alā Sayyidinā Muḥammadin wa `alā Ālihī, Wa khalliqnā bi akhlāqihi ‘l-jamīlah.

and send prayers upon, salute and bless our Master Muḥammad and his Family and imbue us with his beautiful character!

اَللَّهُمَّ صَلِّ وَسَلِّمْ وَبَارِكْ عَلَى سَيِّدِنَا مُحَمَّدٍ وَعَلَى أٰلِه وَهَبْ لَنَا قَلْبًا شَكُوْرًا .

Allāhumma ṣalli wa sallim wa bārik `alā Sayyidinā Muḥammadin wa `alā Ālihī, Wa hab lanā qalban shakūrā.

O Allah send prayers upon, salute and bless our Master Muḥammad and his Family and bestow upon us grateful hearts!!

49

وَصَلِّ وَسَلِّمْ وَ بَارِكْ عَلَى سَيِّدِنَا مُحَمَّدٍ وَعَلَى أٰلِهٖ وَاجْعَلْ سَعْيَنَا مَشْكُوْرًا .

Wa ṣalli wa sallim wa bārik `alā Sayyidinā Muḥammadin wa `alā Ālihī, Wa ‘j`al sa`yanā mashkūrā.

and send prayers upon, salute and bless our Master Muḥammad and his Family and make our striving appreciated!

وَصَلِّ وَسَلِّمْ وَ بَارِكْ عَلَى سَيِّدِنَا مُحَمَّدٍ وَعَلَى أٰلِهٖ وَلَقِّنَا نَضْرَةً وَسُرُوْرًا .

Wa ṣalli wa sallim wa bārik `alā Sayyidinā Muḥammadin wa `alā Ālihī, Wa laqqinā naḍratan wa surūrā.

and send prayers upon, salute and bless our Master Muḥammad and his Family and give us splendor and happiness!

وَصَلِّ وَسَلِّمْ وَ بَارِكْ عَلَى سَيِّدِنَا مُحَمَّدٍ وَعَلَى أٰلِهٖ وَأَلْقِ عَلَيْنَا مِنْكَ مَحَبَّةً وَنُوْرًا.

Wa ṣalli wa sallim wa bārik `alā Sayyidinā Muḥammadin wa `alā Ālihī, Wa alqi `alaynā minka maḥabbatan wa Nūrā.

and send prayers upon, salute and bless our Master Muḥammad and his Family and confer upon us from love and light from You!

50

وَصَلِّ وَسَلِّمْ وَ بَارِكْ عَلَى سَيِّدِنَا مُحَمَّدٍ وَعَلَى أٰلِهٖ وَهَبْ لَنَا سِرًّا بِالْأَسْرَارِ مَسْرُوْرًا .

Wa ṣalli wa sallim wa bārik `alā Sayyidinā Muḥammadin wa `alā Ālihī, Wa hab lanā sirran bi ‘l-asrāri masrūrā.

and send prayers upon, salute and bless our Master Muḥammad and his Family and bestow upon us a secret from the secrets of happiness!

اَللَّهُمَّ صَلِّ وَسَلِّمْ وَ بَارِكْ عَلَى سَيِّدِنَا مُحَمَّدٍ وَعَلَى أٰلِهٖ الصَّادِقِ الْأَمِيْنِ .

Allāhumma ṣalli wa sallim wa bārik `alā Sayyidinā Muḥammadin wa `alā Ālihī, Aṣ-Ṣādiqi ‘l-Amīn.

O Allah send prayers upon, salute and bless our Master Muḥammad and his Family the truthful and trustworthy!

وَصَلِّ وَسَلِّمْ وَبَارِكْ عَلَى سَيِّدِنَا مُحَمَّدٍ وَعَلَى أٰلِهٖ الَّذِيْ جَاءَ بِالْحَقِّ الْمُبِيْنِ .

Wa ṣalli wa sallim wa bārik `alā Sayyidinā Muḥammadin wa `alā Ālihī, Alladhī jā’a bi ‘l-Haqqi ‘l-Mubīn.

and send prayers upon, salute and bless our Master Muḥammad and his Family the one who came with manifest truth!

51

وَصَلِّ وَسَلِّمْ وَ بَارِكْ عَلَى سَيِّدِنَا مُحَمَّدٍ وَعَلَى أٰلِهٖ الَّذِيْ أَرْسَلْتَهُ رَحْمَةً لِلْعَالَمِيْنَ .

Wa ṣalli wa sallim wa bārik `alā Sayyidinā Muḥammadin wa `alā Ālihī, Alladhī arsaltahū Raḥmata ‘l li ‘l-Ālamīn.

and send prayers upon, salute and bless our Master Muḥammad and his Family the one You sent as a mercy to the Worlds!

اَللَّهُمَّ صَلِّ وَسَلِّمْ وَبَارِكْ عَلَى سَيِّدِنَا مُحَمَّدٍ وَعَلَى أٰلِهٖ وَعَلَى سَائِرِ أَنْبِيَآئِكَ .

Allāhumma ṣalli wa sallim wa bārik `alā Sayyidinā Muḥammadin wa `alā Ālihī, Wa `alā sā’iri anbiyā’ik.

O Allah send prayers upon, salute and bless our Master Muḥammad and his Family and all Your Prophets!

52

وَصَلِّ وَسَلِّمْ وَ بَارِكْ عَلَى سَيِّدِنَا مُحَمَّدٍ وَعَلَى أٰلِهٖ وَعَلَى مَلٰٓئِكَتِكَ وَ أَوْلِيَائِكَ مِنْ أَهْلِ أَرْضِكَ وَسَمَائِكَ عَدَدَ مَا كَانَ وَعَدَدَ مَا يَكُوْنُ وَعَدَدَ مَا هُوَ كَائِنٌ فِيْ عِلْمِ اللّٰهِ أَبَدَ الْأٓبِدِيْنَ وَدَهْرَ الدَّاهِرِيْنَ وَاجْعَلْنَا بِالصَّلَاةِ عَلَيْهِمْ مِنَ الصِّدِّيْقِيْنَ الْأٓمِنِيْنَ يَا رَبَّ الْعَالَمِيْنَ .

Wa ṣalli wa sallim wa bārik `alā Sayyidinā Muḥammadin wa `alā Ālihī, Wa `alā Malā’ikatika wa Awliyā’ika min ahli arḍika wa samā’ik, `Adada mā kāna wa `adada mā yakūnu wa `adada mā huwa kā’inun fī `ilmi ‘llāh, Abada al-ābidīna wa dahra ‘d-dāhirīna, Wa ‘j`alnā bi ‘ṣ-Ṣalāti `alayhim mina ṣ-Ṣiddīqīna ‘l-Āminīna, Yā Rabba ‘l-`Ālamīn.

and send prayers upon, salute and bless our Master Muḥammad and his Family, and Your Angels and Saints from inhabitants of Your earth and sky, to the number of what was, is and will be in Allah's knowledge forever and ever for all eternity and make us, by sending prayers upon them, of the truthful and trustworthy, O Lord of the Worlds!
